# Notice: data behind the computed values in this folder

The numbers in `comparison.json`, `sample_metrics.csv` and `local/details.json`, and the figures
`fig_*.svg`, are computed by `run.py` from the archived measurements of:

R. F. Ubbink, G. Almeida, H. Iziyi, I. du Fossé, R. Verkleij, S. Ganapathy, E. R. H. van Eck,
A. J. Houtepen. Data underlying the article: A Water-free In Situ HF Treatment of InP Quantum
Dots. 4TU.ResearchData, version 1, 2023. doi 10.4121/1370d230-bca3-4af4-af06-1a9f168a4b10.v1.
Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

No data file, data row or spectrum of the dataset is included here. The files hold only values
we computed: quantum yields, peak positions and widths, lifetimes, contrasts, and single
absorbance values at stated wavelengths (387, 330 and 450 nm, and the 680-700 nm baseline) that
enter those calculations. The figures plot these computed values, drawn by our own code. Plots
of the spectra themselves go to `local/`, which git ignores.

**Committed.** Only `comparison.json`. It holds the 23 checks (22 units) with one computed value
each against the reported value, the verdict, and aggregates: which contrasts are linked or
confounded and whether their PLQY change is measurable (sample identifiers, no values), the
number of samples meeting each quality-screen cell, and the PLQY, yield and stock change of the
hypothetical extension. It carries the attribution in its `data_source` block. The text of
`../README.md`, `../comparison.md` and `../extension.md` also quotes individual computed values
where a result or an explanation needs them.

**Not committed, pending the owner's decision.** `sample_metrics.csv` (one row per sample),
`local/details.json` (per-sample values behind the bound checks, diagnostics, contrast values,
quality-screen membership, extension rows) and the figures are regenerated by `run.py` and
git-ignored. Whether values derived this closely from a CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 dataset may be published
here under CC BY 4.0 is open for the owner (the licence also covers database rights); the
decision also covers the values quoted in the text. If the answer is yes, these files can be
committed; if not, they stay local and the quoted values are removed. `../source.yaml`
(`reuse_terms`, frozen in 75775d98) describes the earlier plan to commit per-sample values and
figures; this notice states what is committed now.

The reported numbers compared against come from the article (Chem. Mater. 2022, 34, 10093,
doi 10.1021/acs.chemmater.2c02800, CC BY 4.0) and its supporting information, restated as
facts in `../source.yaml`.
