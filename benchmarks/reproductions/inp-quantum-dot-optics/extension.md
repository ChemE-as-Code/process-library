# HYPOTHETICAL design extension (not validated): treatment time for a blue-pumped colour-conversion layer

This is a design calculation built on the measured 150 °C series (one session, 2021-12-08). It is
not a reproduction, nothing in the source tests it, and it must not be used as design data.
The change is in `results/comparison.json` under `extension`; the rows and the aliquot-volume
sensitivity are in `results/local/details.json` under `extension`. That file and the figure
`results/fig_extension.svg` are git-ignored and regenerated by `run.py` (`results/NOTICE.md`).

## Question

A display layer converts a 450 nm blue pump to green with in situ HF treated InP cores. The layer
is to absorb 90 % of the pump (absorbance 1.0 at 450 nm). How much untreated QD stock does each
square metre need, and what fraction of the blue photons comes out green, when the treatment at
150 °C is stopped after 5 min instead of 60 min?

## Model (inputs.yaml, `extension`)

- Absorbance at 450 nm retained, per starting QD (R₄₅₀): absorbance at 450 nm of an aliquot over
  that of the 0 min aliquot, at equal dilution (assumed; Figure 1C is labelled absolute
  absorbance). This is an absorbance at the pump wavelength, not a material balance. The first
  exciton moves from 476.8 nm to 457 nm at 5 min, close to the pump, so R₄₅₀ is higher than the
  ratio at 330 nm (R₃₃₀), where the extinction coefficient is stated. R₃₃₀ is the closer measure
  of InP kept and is reported alongside.
- The extinction coefficient of the untreated QDs at 450 nm is 0.45 cm⁻¹ µM⁻¹ (stated by the
  source at 330 nm) scaled by us with the measured 450/330 nm ratio of the 0 min aliquot:
  9.3 × 10⁴ M⁻¹ cm⁻¹ per QD.
- Starting QDs per area: n = A₄₅₀ / (1000 · ε₄₅₀ · R₄₅₀) mol cm⁻², with R₄₅₀ the retained fraction.
  Standard batch: 41 nmol QDs (Methods).
- Blue-to-green photon yield: (1 − 10^−A₄₅₀) × PLQY. This ignores reabsorption, scattering,
  outcoupling and any change of the QDs in a solid matrix.

## Result

| Treatment at 150 °C | PLQY | A(450 nm) retained | A(330 nm) retained | Starting QDs per m² | 41 nmol batches per m² | Blue → green |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 60 min (base) | 55.9 % | 34.6 % | 28.5 % | 310 µmol | 7550 | 50.3 % |
| 5 min (changed input) | 42.4 % | 36.4 % | 29.0 % | 295 µmol | 7190 | 38.2 % |
| 0 min (untreated, for scale) | 0.09 % | 100 % | 100 % | 107 µmol | 2620 | 0.08 % |

Volume bias (`extension.aliquot_volume_bias`). The Methods add 90 µL of octylamine and 90 µL of
benzoyl fluoride solution after the QDs are diluted to 850 µL. The 0 min aliquot is read as taken
before benzoyl fluoride (mapping.csv). If all aliquots were handled alike, the 0 min aliquot is
1.10 times (octylamine already added) to 1.21 times (neither added) more concentrated than the
later ones. The retained fractions then rise by that factor and the stock per m² falls by it:

| Treatment at 150 °C | A(450 nm) retained | A(330 nm) retained | Starting QDs per m² | 41 nmol batches per m² |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 60 min | 38.0-42.0 % | 31.2-34.5 % | 256-283 µmol | 6230-6890 |
| 5 min | 39.9-44.1 % | 31.8-35.2 % | 243-269 µmol | 5930-6560 |

The table above keeps equal dilution, as frozen in inputs.yaml. The bias cancels in the 5 against
60 min comparison.

**Engineering consequence.** Within the first 5 min, etching removes about 64 % of the 450 nm
absorbance (56-60 % with the volume bias) and about 71 % of the 330 nm absorbance (65-68 %), the
closer measure of InP lost. After that both barely change (A₄₅₀ 36.4 % → 34.6 %, A₃₃₀ 29.0 % →
28.5 % retained), while the PLQY rises from 42 % to 56 %. Stopping at 5 min therefore saves only
4.8 % of the starting stock per square metre and costs 12.2 percentage points of blue-to-green
conversion. That is about a quarter of the green output (38.2 against 50.3 photons per 100 blue
photons). In the measured range, the extra 55 min at 150 °C costs reactor time and almost no
material. On both absorbance ratios, the material cost of the treatment is set in the first
minutes. This series has a single benzoyl fluoride dose, so the role of the dose is not tested
here; the source states that a higher dose gives a larger absorption drop (SI Figure S4 caption,
Results).

## Flags (out of range, extrapolation, evidence)

- One run, one session, no replicate. The 30 and 60 min PLQYs differ by 1.8 pp, below the 5 pp
  measurement band, so a trend between 30 and 60 min is not resolved.
- Equal dilution of aliquots is assumed, not documented. The documented reagent additions alone
  make the 0 min aliquot 1.10-1.21 times more concentrated if aliquots were handled alike (range
  above). Any other difference in aliquot dilution changes the retained fractions and the stock
  per m² in proportion. The washed sample is excluded because its redispersion volume is unknown.
- The layer absorbance at 540 nm would be about 0.18-0.21 for A₄₅₀ = 1. Reabsorption of the
  green emission is therefore not negligible, and the conversion values are upper bounds.
- Solution PLQY is taken for a solid layer. Film formation, ligand loss and matrix effects are
  outside the data.
- Temperatures other than 150 °C, other doses, ZnCl2, or stock and batch sizes beyond 41 nmol
  are extrapolation. The source itself notes that the gaseous HF makes kinetics and scale-up
  hard to control. No scale-up claim is made.
- The extinction coefficient comes from a model (Maxwell-Garnett), as the source states. Its
  uncertainty passes directly into the stock per m², but not into the 5 against 60 min comparison.
