## Limitations

- gate-to-gate: no upstream supply chain
- construction (infrastructure, equipment) not included
- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: cutoff:small-pumps-and-agitator (reflux pumps of C-101, C-201 and C-202, the LP bottoms pump and the R-101 agitator are not in the flowsheet's power feed; together about 25 kW x 8000 h/a (ASSUMED order of magnitude, not sized, D-008): below 1 % of the plant's energy input (steam 6.3 MW))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:storage-tanks (BDO and THF tanks are outside the battery limit (design basis): their breathing losses belong to the storage, not to this plant boundary (D-011))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:fugitive-leaks (fugitive THF from flanges, seals and valves is not estimated, not claimed negligible: an equipment-leak estimate needs the P&ID component counts, which do not exist at the concept stage; to become an emission_estimates entry once P&IDs are drawn (DESIGN.md I-07, D-011))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:condenser-vents (the vents of the reactor overhead condenser E-101 and the LP condenser E-205 carry the inert gas of the pressure control (nitrogen blanket) with THF at its vapour pressure at the condenser outlet; no inert flow is set at the concept stage, so the THF vented is not estimated, not claimed negligible (DESIGN.md I-07))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:wastewater-effluent (the process water goes to the site wastewater treatment (product:WW with its treatment); the emissions to water after treatment belong to that treatment background, not to this foreground (D-011))
