## Limitations

- gate-to-gate: no upstream supply chain
- construction (infrastructure, equipment) not included
- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: cutoff:fugitive-CH4 (no P&ID component count at the concept stage; ASSUMED screening amount of 0.1 % of the methane processed (S1: 64 870 kg/h CH4 x 0.001 x 8000 h/a), the upper end of the 0.05-0.1 % suggested by the design review (R-03). Below 1 % of the input mass, but methane has a high characterisation factor: about 0.020 kg CO2-eq per kg sales gas allocated (GWP100 29.8), roughly twice the direct GHG of the inventory (D-017))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:leaks (fugitive methane and hydrocarbons from valves, flanges, compressor seals and the TEG/amine pumps are not estimated: no P&ID take-off at the concept stage. In gas processing they are a material methane source; to be added with the P&IDs and the EPA leak protocol factors (D-014); screening amount for the cut-off check in cutoff:fugitive-CH4 (D-017))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:non-routine (blowdowns, compressor starts, mole sieve bed switching losses, pigging and flaring at upsets are not estimated at the concept stage (D-014))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:tanks (no hydrocarbon storage: NGL leaves by pipeline and there is no condensate (no C4+ in the feed); amine, TEG and produced-water tanks breathe negligible amounts (ASSUMED))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:water (the separated water goes to disposal (a waste treatment, mapped as product:PWATER); its dissolved hydrocarbons and salts are not in the balance (D-004))
