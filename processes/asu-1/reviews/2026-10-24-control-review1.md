# Review: control intent of ASU-1 (loop register, sequences, philosophy, narrative), first review

This is an independent process-control review of branch `stage/basic-control` at ef612f6. The
two commits on top of main 6ea7eb5 are 8bb1311 (loop register, sequences, narrative, control
philosophy, D-094) and ef612f6 (the open points name the HAZOP actions). The review covers
control/loops.yaml, control/sequences.yaml, control/philosophy.md, process/narrative.md and the
DESIGN.md entry D-094. They are judged against D-001 .. D-019, D-024, D-061, D-067, D-069,
D-071, D-076, D-077, D-079, D-083, D-088, D-089 and D-093, the instrument list, the C&E, the
flowsheet design case, the P&IDs (`cheme show`) and the HAZOP drafts H-01 .. H-04.

Checks run at ef612f6:
- `cheme validate --only "cheme.control-*" --skip-generators`: 0 errors and 0 warnings. The
  infos are `control-incomplete` / `control-unresolved` / `control-logic-text` and one
  `control-shared-element` (TICA-3124 / I-04 / TV-3124). The 15 set points that name a stream
  agree with process/flowsheet.yaml.
- `cheme control report --json`: all four control records are `drafted`, and no review record
  covers them.

Verdict: the format, the stream links and most controller actions are right, and open values
are mostly left unresolved. But the records contain structural control conflicts, start-up
sequences in an impossible or unsafe order, a tank set point at the tank's design pressure,
and several statements that contradict the HAZOP or claim protection the design does not give.
**Seven high findings are open.** All records stay PROPOSED. This agent record approves none of
them.

## Checked and fine
- **Controller actions** (convention: direct = the output rises with the measurement; the
  valve's opening direction is separate). These are consistent with the final element and
  its location: FIC-1101, PIC-1103, FIC-1108, FIC-2105, FIC-3106, FIC-3111, FIC-3107, FIC-4105
  reverse; TIC-1105 direct (chilled water on CHW-1001 upstream of C-1101, D-069); LIC-1106 and
  LIC-1114 direct (outlet valves); LIC-1107 reverse (make-up inlet); TIC-2104 reverse
  (thyristor); LIC-3101 direct (LV-3101 outlet); SIC-3108 reverse (nozzles); PIC-3110 direct
  (back-pressure on the waste outlets); PIC-3113 reverse (N2 supply); TICA-3124 direct
  (TV-3124 on CHW-3001, D-069); PIC-4101 direct (IGV draw, then the vent); PICA-4109 / PICA-4110
  direct (vents); PIC-4111 / PIC-4112 reverse (supply valves).
- **Split ranges.** PIC-3110: PV-3110A (C-1102, the normal path for all of S12B, D-067 / D-071)
  first, PV-3110B (bypass vent) above it. PIC-4101: IGV output A, then PV-4101B, as D-019 and
  the instrument list state. Both are physically in the right order. The 50 % points are
  marked ASSUMED.
- **Fail positions given** agree with the design: LV-1114 closed (D-069), TV-3124 open (D-069 /
  D-077), UV-3120 open (D-003), UV-4131 open (D-024). The others are unresolved, not defaulted.
- **Set points and units** against the design case: S2 111,720 kg/h, S3 12 C, S12H 200 C,
  S12A 18,486 kg/h, S18 29,280 kg/h, S6 -178 C (D-061), S16 1.3 bara, S10 470 kg/h, S4E 15 C
  with TAH 18.5 C (D-067), S7 1.15 bara (D-002), S8 31 bara (30 barg given), S9 / S9B
  12,510 kg/h (10,000 Nm3/h). The FIC-3111 minimum of 43 kg/h equals ~30 Nm3/h O2, 0.2 % of the
  product (D-010). The LIC-1114 set point of 13.50 m and the I-03 trip at 13.35 m match
  D-088 (4). `cheme validate` keeps the 15 stream-linked values in step.
- **Coverage.** All 31 controllers of the instrument list (xIC, xICA, UIC) have a loop. The
  four C&E interlocks are referenced by id, and I-03 and I-04 are not restated as loops.
  TICA-3124 / I-04 sharing TV-3124 is declared (`control-shared-element`) with D-077's reason.
- **SEQ-TSA step order** (depressurise, heat, cool through XV-2109 with E-2101 off,
  repressurise, switch) agrees with D-022 and the instrument list.
- **SEQ-SHUTDOWN order** (customers to the backup, product compressors, compander, MAC).
  SEQ-COMPANDER-TRIP's E-3105 dry check before the restart matches D-077 (1).
- Every record carries "PROPOSED ... not approved for implementation", and none claims a
  review or approval.
