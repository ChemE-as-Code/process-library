## Criteria (ASSUMED unless the site plan's criteria say otherwise)

- `heavy_lift_t` 5: Items of 5 t or more are lifted by a crane (mobile on a crane pad, or the building's overhead crane); lighter ones by hoists and small equipment (ASSUMED). (package default)
- `crane_reach_m` 30: Working radius of a mobile crane standing on a crane pad, when the pad gives no reach_m (ASSUMED). (package default)
- `lane_margin_m` 1: Clearance either side of a load in its crane lane (ASSUMED). (package default)
- `lift_clearance_m` 0.5: A load is lifted at least this much over what it passes; anything in its lane higher than the item's top plus this blocks the lift (ASSUMED). (package default)
- `lift_dynamic_factor` 1.1: The load in a lift is the item's weight times a dynamic allowance of 1.1 for hoisting with a mobile crane (ASSUMED; the lifting plan sets it). (package default)
- `lift_rigging_fraction` 0.05: Slings, shackles, spreader and hook block weigh about 5 % of the load (ASSUMED; replace with the rigging's weight from the lifting plan). (package default)
- `lift_rigging_height_m` 3: Height of the rigging between the hook and the top of the load (slings and spreader, ASSUMED). (package default)
- `lift_chart_utilisation` 0.75: A lift may use at most 75 % of the crane's chart capacity at its working radius; above that it is a critical lift with its own plan (ASSUMED; the site's lifting procedure sets the limit). (package default)

Not covered: ground bearing under the crane and its outriggers, the rigging design, the crane's own clearance to overhead lines and structures while slewing, wind, tandem lifts and tailing: the lifting plan (competent person, crane contractor).
