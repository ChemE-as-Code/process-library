# Source attribution

The worker calls the installed QSDsan and EXPOsan dependencies. The model and
initial conditions are supplied by EXPOsan 1.5.3 (`exposan/bsm1/system.py`,
ASM1 CSTR branch) from the Quantitative Sustainable Design Group and contributors.
The license files under `licenses/` reproduce the upstream QSDsan and EXPOsan
notices. The example does not vendor simulator sources or initialization files.

The diagrams are generated from the authored process definition. The reference
JSON records final values of the teaching simulation. Local worker histories are
ignored and remain with their local runs.
