## Verdict

**Fix before merge (agent review; not a sign-off).** Revision E is a clean, drawing-only change.
It moves three connectors to the edge without a crossing, and the BFD and PFD of the 1.11.0 pin
are unchanged in content.

However, the branch makes gate drawing-clean fail on CD-x01 (#1, medium). That needs the
owner's decision, a drawn route with an accepted crossing or an accepted connector, before the
branch is merged. Otherwise it would turn a passing basic-stage gate into a failing one on
main.

#2 (low) asks for one convention for the capped vents, drains and pump-outs, which would also
restore AV-x11 and DR-x03 to a reading that matches their function. #3 (low) is two wording
slips.

No critical or high finding is open. Round 1 #18 and the seven low findings of round 3 stay
open, unchanged by this branch. A human design review is still required before sign-off, and
the branch's merge is pending.
