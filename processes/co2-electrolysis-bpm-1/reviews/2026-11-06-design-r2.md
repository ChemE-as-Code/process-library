## Engineering plausibility of the new set points (Skid A, `process/flowsheet.yaml`)

- Cathode: CO2-x01 (S101, S103) 120 kPa(a), CG-x01 (S104) 110 kPa(a): about 10 kPa across
  the stacks. PIA-x23 trip 0.35 bar(g) = 136 kPa(a) at the outlet; PSE-x01 0.5 bar(g) on the
  inlet header; stacks and V-x21 designed for 1.0 bar(g). Normal operating ratio at the disc
  (0.19 / 0.5 bar(g)) is 0.38. The trip-to-burst margin at the disc is #6.
- Discharge: CG-x03 600 kPa(a) (5.0 bar(g)), PAHH 5.8, hydraulic limit 6.5 and design
  7.0 bar(g). The spacing is consistent.
- Membrane: AN-x03 (S114) 141.3 kPa(a) in, AN-x04 (S115) 101.3 kPa(a) out; cathode 120 in,
  110 out. Local differences (anolyte minus cathode) +21.3 kPa at the inlet end and -8.7 kPa at
  the outlet end. PIA-x13 minus PIA-x23 = +31.3 kPa (#3).
- Total CO2: FIC-x04 3.30 kg/h = S101 0.356 + S103 2.942; FV-x02 has to span 0.36 to 3.3 kg/h
  (about 9:1, stated in D-062). I-x15 at 50 %. Consistent.
- H2 in O2: AH 0.4 vol%, AHH 1.0 vol% against an LFL of about 4 vol%. Consistent with the
  usual 10 % / 25 % LFL practice.
- Stack current: 4 x 20 A = 80 A bus, IAHH 24 A per stack. Consistent with JIC-x01.
- P-202: S221 (KOH-204) 400 kPa(a), the integral relief 3.0 bar(g), BPV-202 1.5 bar(g) (#11).
