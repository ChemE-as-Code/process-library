  decision record. The bottom edge is allowed by the ADR (the rule does not check the side; the
  convention lets header rows use the top and bottom edges), and the arrow is 9 mm above the title
  block, inside the 10 mm tolerance. **The reason is sound.** Its attribution to the owner is
