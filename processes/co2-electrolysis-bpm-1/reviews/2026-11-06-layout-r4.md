- **The tail gas overhead (r3#3).**
  - TG-x01 is at 2.3 m, with its pipe bottom at 2.289 m, above the 2.1 m walkway headroom. It
    stands on 16 posts with their tops at 2.289 m, at a pitch of 1.7 m or less.
  - The ways north and east from ST-x2's foot no longer cross the line at grade. A-100 and A-200
    now hold the east legs (x 15.2 m and 30.2 m, inside 16.3 m and 31.3 m) and the panels.
  - **r3#3 closed by cb831d08.** The change has three side-effects that no rule reported:
    - **Trays below headroom (#1).** To pass under the line at 2.3 m, core re-routed
      PT-M-K-131 and PT-M-K-231 from 2.5 m to 1.75 m. It also dropped PT-DC-E101D and
      PT-DC-E201D from 3.1 m to 1.85 m. At d4b02bf no level tray run was lower than 2.1 m; there
      are now six (table in #1). D-065's Consequence does not mention this.
    - **Two liquid traps in a wet hydrogen line (#2).** The inverted U has low points at the
      riser foot by X-x31/N4 and at the drop into X-x32/N1. The flowsheet's S109 is 48 mol% H2
      and 19.6 mol% H2O at 25 degC and 110 kPa, against about 2.9 mol% at saturation. The line
      therefore carries free water into both pockets, outdoors on the open skid. D-061 rejects a
      liquid seal because it "freezes on the open skid". No drain is drawn on TG-x01.
    - **The east leg is beside the flue.** The drop runs at x 15.2 m from 2.3 m to 0.3 m, 0.5 m
      from FG-x01's riser (x 14.7 m, 1.517 to 3.217 m). The flowsheet's flue temperature is a
      placeholder 288 K (ASSUMED), so the heat on the line is not assessed (#2).

  - With the new route, core's in-line pitch now puts the items on the riser at (8.5, 10.8):
