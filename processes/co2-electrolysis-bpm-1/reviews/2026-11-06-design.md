## Engineering plausibility (Skid A, design case, `cheme process streams`)

- Charge: 4 stacks x 7 cells at 20 A (200 mA/cm2 on 100 cm2), bus 80 A. 0.100 kg/h CO
  delivered at recovery 0.90 is 3.97 mol/h formed; 28 x 20 A x FE / 2F gives FE CO 0.38, with
  FE H2 0.56 (D-012) a sum of 0.94. Consistent with D-006.
- O2: 28 x 20 A / 4F = 5.2 mol/h = 0.167 kg/h; S116 0.158 kg/h (water and the carbonate route
  account for the rest). Consistent.
- Stack heat: 560 A x (5.37 - about 1.4 V thermoneutral) = 2.2 kW; cooler 705.6 kg/h x 3.95
  kJ/(kg K) x 2.88 K = 2.23 kW (S113/S114, D-012). Consistent.
- Anolyte DN 25: 11.2 L/min, about 0.34 m/s. Cathode gas DN 15: S106 about 3.26 kg/h, mostly
  CO2, about 78 mol/h, 1.76 m3/h at 110 kPa(a) and 298 K (29 L/min), about 2.4 m/s. Both
  reasonable.
- The cathode CO2: S101 fresh 0.356 kg/h, S103 PSA recycle 2.942 kg/h, so FIC-x02 sets 11 % of
  the 3.30 kg/h (28 SLPM, 10 sccm/cm2) the FE was measured at; see finding #8.
- Pressures: CO2 feed 120 kPa(a), cathode outlet 110 kPa(a), anolyte 141.3 kPa(a) in and
  101.3 kPa(a) out of the stacks; the pressure difference across the membrane changes sign
  along the stack (finding #3).
- D-058: T-101 1,176 L to the pH 12.2 trip at H, design basis `threshold.ph: 12` kept and
  `threshold.trip.ph: 12.2` added; I-101, AI-101 (AAL 12.5), SEQ-A-FILL/STOP and the equipment
  list agree. The frozen files are untouched; erratum X-2 carries the basis (not re-derived here).

## Verdict

**Fix.** The owner's decisions are carried through the sheets, lists, control records and
DESIGN.md consistently, the regenerated P&IDs pass drawing-clean without the drift
acceptances, and the tail-gas, drain and Ex decisions are sound as far as they go. But for basic
engineering the design still has no overpressure protection on a positive-displacement
compressor and a membrane-limited cathode (#1). A stopped compressor or lost instrument air
leaves the current on against closed outlets (#2), and the oxygen side has no H2-in-O2
measurement (#3). These three high findings must be closed, or turned into recorded design
decisions with their reasons, before a basic sign-off. The medium findings should be answered
in the same revision or deferred explicitly in DESIGN.md. A human design review is still
required.
