## Engineering plausibility of the revision D set points (Skid A)

- **Cathode high-high.** PIA-116 sits on the feed header beside PSE-101. Normal pressure there
  is about 0.19 bar(g) (120 kPa(a)). The trip is at 0.35 bar(g) and the disc bursts at
  0.5 bar(g) ±0.05 (ASSUMED), so the gap at the same point is 0.15 bar (at least 0.10 bar after
  the disc's tolerance). The operating ratio is 0.38 at the normal pressure and 0.70 at the trip.
  The 0.70 equals the stated maximum of 0.7, so the relief study must pick a disc type that
  allows it (recorded in `piping/special-items.yaml`).
- **Membrane, inlet end.** PIA-113 (AN-103, 141.3 kPa(a)) minus PIA-116 (120 kPa(a)) is about
  +21 kPa.
- **Membrane, outlet end.** PIA-115 (AN-104 at stack D, about 101.3 kPa(a)) minus PIA-123
  (CG-101, 110 kPa(a)) is about −9 kPa.
- **Membrane window (ASSUMED).** Alarms at +45 / −15 kPa, trips at +60 / −20 kPa. The design
  point is 6 kPa from the low alarm and 11 kPa from the low trip at the outlet end (#3).
- **Stop step.** The residual pressure is 106 kPa(a) on PIC-121. With P-101 circulating, the
  ends read +35 and −5 kPa, inside the window, as D-067 says. With P-101 stopped they do not
  (#3).
- **P-202.** The relief is at 4.0 bar(g). The discharge with BPV-202 at 1.5 bar(g) is about
  1.5 to 2 bar(g) (S221 is 3.0 bar(g), the stated upper bound), and the design is 6.0 bar(g).
  The spacing is consistent.
