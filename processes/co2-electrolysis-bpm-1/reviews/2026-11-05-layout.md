# Basic layout review of the pilot skids: 3D model, routes, access, zones, lifting, take-off, cabling

Branch `feat/co2-bpm-3d`, reviewed at 05446ad. This is the independent layout review that gate
`layout-review` and the `drawings` requirement of `layout-3d` ask for (DESIGN.md D-040, D-046).
It covers the 1,000 h design of both skids: the notional plot, the two 20 ft frames, placement,
zones, the routed DN 25 lines and the pins, the stacks' hand lift (D-042), the lifting study
(D-043), the take-off (D-044), the cabling as far as it touches the layout (D-045), and the
plans and sections in `figures/skids/`. The 10,000 h scenario is reported as infeasible
(D-015, D-021) and is looked at only where its figures are in scope. The reviewer did not build
this model.

**This is an agent review. A human layout review is still required** before the layout is
signed off or merged. This record does not replace that review.

## Engineering checks

- **Model and routes.** Every DN 25 line is routed, no clash, no `direct` fallback. I checked
  every leg of every routed pipe in the model: the shortest leg between two elbows is 0.081 m
  (AN-204 S4) and the shortest end leg 0.07 m (AN-201 S1). So D-044's statement that every elbow
  can be built (76 mm between elbows, 38 mm at an end, DN 25 long radius) holds. KOH-101 and
  SP-101 still have a zero-length leg from the router, but it adds no elbow.
- **Elevation profiles.** The anolyte loop runs 0.20 m (supply header) to 0.60 m (return header).
  AN-x03 S1 rises to 0.90 m. AN-104 S4 rises to 2.23 m over T-101 and AN-204 S4 to 1.18 m over
  T-201. The drains leave the tanks at 0.30 m (T-101) and 0.25 m (T-201). The low point of each
  loop is therefore below the drain, and the high points have no vent (finding #4).
- **Stack hand lift (D-042, the open question put to this review).** The regen check is correct
  for what it checks: the lift column above each stack is clear, and the carry height is 1.275 m
  above grade, set by the guard rail. It does not check whether a person can reach the stack.
  Stack centres are at y 8.70 m. The south frame edge and rail are at y 7.50 m, so the
  horizontal reach is about 1.2 m from the rail and more from the body. The north edge is
  1.15 m from the stack faces, behind the 1.8 m PSA and the oxidiser. The deck between the rows
  holds the headers and the step-over proposals; it has no standing space. The answer to D-042's
  question is **no** (finding #1).
- **Zones and ignition sources.** Core generates the zone spheres at the item centres, radius
  1 m. I measured from the model (finding #2, finding #3):
  - X-132's box is 0.50 m from the E-101C sphere centre and 0.70 m from the X-131 sphere centre,
    so the oxidiser flame is inside Z-HAZ-A. Skid B is the same, shifted 15 m.
  - U-101's box is 1.12 m from the E-101D centre, 1.30 m from X-132 and 1.36 m from K-131.
  - With a source at the stack's north end plate (gas connections at about (9.97, 8.79, 0.28)),
    U-101 is 1.02 m away. A flange on X-132's east face would be 1.00 m away.
- **Separations.** X-131 to X-231 is 14 m (6 m required). The skids are 8.9 m apart and the
  zones do not overlap. The skid areas stand 2.5 m off the road (1.5 m required). Turning the
  spacing table off inside a frame is a reasonable concept-stage choice.
- **Access and egress.** Each deck has two remote ways off (ST-x1, ST-x2), outside the zone.
  Every manual valve and transmitter is within reach from grade or the deck. The rack ladders
  are 2.8 m, uncaged.
- **Acceptances in checks/checks.yaml left for this review.** Judged as follows:
  - Pump access boxes (P-101, P-201, P-202): **agreed.** The pumps stand 0.28 to 0.33 m inside
    the south edge and are reachable from grade.
  - KOH-101 at 1.16 m and AN-104 S4 at about 0.43 / 1.9 m clear over the deck: **agreed on the
    premise that the 0.15 m deck is not a walkway.** That premise is also what makes finding #1
    a defect.
  - The 12 tray-zone findings: **agreed as an assumption only.** They stand until the area
    classification and the Ex selection (D-031 item 12) confirm them.
- **Step-overs (D-040).** Declining all 30 is **agreed**: a 0.15 m deck worked from grade needs
  none. They stay in the model, the drawings and the take-off (finding #11).
- **Lifting (D-043).**
  - The arithmetic of the unit lifts re-reads correctly from the study: 3.261 t x 1.1 x 1.05 =
    3.767 t gross, / 0.75 = 5.02 t at 7.22 m, 27.2 t m.
  - The weights are all estimates and are marked ASSUMED.
  - The pads and the T-101 route are not workable as declared (findings #6, #7).
- **Electrical (D-045), layout side.**
  - IT-JB-201's 2.1 m aisle run is not accepted (finding #9).
  - The DC trays run within 0.35 to 0.47 m of the FG-x01 flue outlets (finding #5).
  - Two DC trays overhang the frame by up to 0.1 m and are site-fitted. Noted, no finding.
- **Take-off (D-044).** Re-read against `estimate/mto.md`. What it leaves out is stated. PR-1
  makes up most of the steel (finding #12).
- **Sources used by this review** (from the reviewer's knowledge; not checked against the
  published text in this review):
  1. The revised NIOSH lifting equation (Waters et al., Ergonomics 36 (1993) 749-776). The
     horizontal multiplier is 25/H (cm) and becomes 0 beyond H = 63 cm, so any lift at more
     than 63 cm reach gets a recommended weight limit of zero.
  2. EN ISO 14122-3. A guard rail is required where the fall height exceeds 500 mm. A 0.15 m
     deck does not need one.
- **No third-party content** in the reviewed files. The model, routes, figures and tables are
  generated from the project's own YAML.

## Verdict

**fix.** The model is current, routed, clash-free and consistent with its lists, and the
take-off, lifting study and cable schedule are current. The authors document their assumptions
thoroughly. Two high findings are open: the stacks cannot be reached for their hand replacement
(#1), and the oxidiser flame stands inside zone 2 (#2). There are also six medium and four low
findings. With this record, gate `layout-3d` gets its review record, but the layout should not
be signed off until #1 and #2 are resolved.

**A human layout review is still required.** This agent review does not replace it. Merge
pending: not merged, no sign-off.
