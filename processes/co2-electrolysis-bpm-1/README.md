## This case

The case is CO2 electrolysis to CO, grounded in the measured water dissociation efficiencies of
Prats Vergel, Vermaas, Burdyny et al. (Nature Chemical Engineering 2025; 4TU dataset v1,
CC BY 4.0, see `NOTICE.md`). The tracker is ChemE-as-Code/core#136; the measured cell is #329.
#330 designs two CO2-to-CO pilot skids from it, for a campaign of 1,000 h and of 10,000 h:

- **Skid A** fills its reservoir once with 1 M KOH, sized so that the anolyte reaches the pH
  threshold 12 at the end of the campaign;
- **Skid B** holds a 50 L reservoir at a steady composition by continuous 5 M KOH dosing, a
  bleed and make-up water.

Both deliver 0.100 kg/h CO (ASSUMED pilot duty, recovery 0.90) from four stacks of seven
100 cm2 cells on a 48 V bus, each skid on one 20 ft frame (ASSUMED envelope). Every number is a
concept-stage result on ASSUMED inputs, and FE, WDE, U and kWh/kg are held constant by
assumption, not supported by measurement: the measured runs last 1 h in fresh 1 M KOH
(`DESIGN.md` D-014).

Rerun the skids, from this folder (about 2 min at 1,000 h, 4 min at 10,000 h):

```sh
cheme run scripts/regen.py --duration 1000 --uncertainty                         # the design, in place
cheme run scripts/regen.py --duration 10000 --uncertainty --scratch /tmp/s10000  # records and figures only
cheme run scripts/regen.py --check                                               # both, compared (exit 3 on a difference)
```

Keep the `--scratch` copy outside the git repository: inside it, `cheme validate` resolves the
P&ID generators against the repository and reports them missing, and that finding lands in the
record.

The measured series and the bench cell:

```sh
cheme run scripts/adapt_4tu.py
cheme experiments electrochem experiments/electrochemistry/rbpm-1m-koh.yaml --plot figures/
cheme process cell
cheme run scripts/cell_section.py
```

At the measured mean the longest campaign whose one-fill reservoir fits the frame is about
2,550 h (rounded from E2 of `results/expected.yaml`). Skid B does not grow
with the campaign, but its continuous dosing uses 1.8 times the KOH of Skid A per kg CO
(the floor is 0.648 kg per kg delivered). The FE at the meter's 20 degC basis would need
4 x 8 cells. At equal CO, on/off operation (24 h, half on) uses the same charge and KOH as
Skid A's continuous duty, and part load (12 h at 200, 12 h at 100 mA/cm2) 7.6 % more charge
and 1.47 times the hydroxide deficit (`DESIGN.md` D-013).

The comparison with the published results is held out in
`benchmarks/reproductions/vermaas-rbpm-wde/` and is not used here (ADR 0032).

The life cycle inventory of both skids (#331, `lca/README.md`, `DESIGN.md` D-024 to D-027) is
reconciled with the duty totals at both durations: per kg CO delivered about 34.6 kWh of AC
power, 3.54 kg of CO2 fed and 1.71 kg vented, and KOH 0.65 kg (Skid A) or 1.20 kg (Skid B). Its
climate screening on the Ember 2025 NL grid gives about 10.5 kg CO2e per kg CO for either skid,
a partial screening carbon footprint: the CO2 supply, KOH (with the dilution water of its
solutions), the alkaline wastes (whose carbonate releases about 0.25 kg of fossil CO2 per kg CO
when neutralised), the cooling water service, the stack parts (membrane, Ag, carbon paper, Ni
foam) and construction have no open factor and are listed as gaps, so which skid is lower is not
determined.

## How this repository works

- **This repository holds your plant**: drawings, lists, decisions. It does not hold the tools.
- **The tools come from [`cheme-as-code`](https://github.com/ChemE-as-Code/core)**, installed once on
  your computer (`uv tool install git+https://github.com/ChemE-as-Code/core.git`). Every check,
  drawing and generated file is made by the `cheme` command, so you need it to work here. The
  continuous integration (CI) in `.github/workflows/` installs it too.
- **The skills in `.claude/skills/` are copies** of the ones in cheme-as-code, so that Claude Code
  finds them here. After upgrading (`uv tool upgrade cheme-as-code`), run `cheme skills update` to
  refresh them; `cheme skills status` shows which are out of date. A skill you changed here is
  kept.
- **While cheme-as-code is private**, you need read access to it, and the CI needs the secrets
  named in `.github/workflows/validate.yml`.

## Quick start

```sh
uv tool install git+https://github.com/ChemE-as-Code/core.git   # once: the cheme command
cheme git-setup                 # once per clone: readable P&ID diffs + pre-commit hook
cheme validate                  # after every change
```

To look at the plant in a browser (P&IDs, streams, 3D layout), install the Viewer extra with
`uv tool install 'cheme-as-code[serve] @ git+https://github.com/ChemE-as-Code/core.git'`, run
`cheme serve` here, and see the [Viewer documentation](https://docs.cheme-as-code.org/viewer/).

The 3D model of the skids (`layout/plant-model.yaml`, the 1,000 h design) opens in the
viewer's **Layout 3D** page of `cheme serve`, with its findings beside it; `cheme export-static
build/snapshot` writes the same as static files (`manifest.json` and `data/`) for a viewer
without a server. glTF (and STL) is exported from that page by hand: core 1.5.0 has no
headless glTF export, so no `.glb` is committed (D-041). The 10,000 h model exists only in a
`--scratch` copy of regen; its plans and sections are in `figures/skids/10000h/`.
