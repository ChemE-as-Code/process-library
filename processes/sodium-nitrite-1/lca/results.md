## Limitations

- gate-to-gate: no upstream supply chain
- construction (infrastructure, equipment) not included
- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:no-combustion (No fuel is fired inside the boundary: the burner oxidises ammonia catalytically (its products are all in the balance, S5) and the steam comes from the WHB or the site header (the header's combustion is in its background, feed:LPS))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:non-routine (start-up (gauze ignition, absorber filling), shutdown and burner trips release ammonia, NOx and N2O; not estimated at the concept stage (D-013))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:dust (NaNO2 and NaNO3 dust leaving the dryer filters is not estimated at the concept stage (no filter specification); to be added with the dryer datasheets)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:dissolver (the soda ash dissolver holds a non-volatile aqueous Na2CO3 solution near 45 degC: no volatile component to breathe out; soda dust at the feeder is filtered (ASSUMED))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:leaks (fugitive NH3 and NOx from flanges and seals are not estimated at the concept stage; to be added with the P&IDs)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:platinum (platinum and rhodium lost from the gauze (partly recovered in filters) are a material input and particulate emission not quantified: no open loss rate for nitrite burners was found (open input, D-013))
