- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:vacuum-vent (the C-204 vacuum system vents non-condensables with traces of DIPB and cumene; not quantified (no vacuum system designed, D-008); the Versalis brochure names vacuum pump vents and oily water as the only releases (PDF p. 3))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:oily-water (no process water is fed or formed in the model; the oily water the Versalis brochure names (feed-water carry-over, drains) is not quantified (I-07))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:catalyst-regeneration (ex-situ regeneration of the zeolite (coke burn-off) every few years and the catalyst charge are not quantified: no open charge or life figure for this design (the brochure gives a run length of up to 8 years, no mass); open gap, not negligible by evidence)
