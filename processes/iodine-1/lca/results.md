## Limitations

- gate-to-gate: no upstream supply chain
- construction (infrastructure, equipment) not included
- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:fugitive-leaks (Not estimated in this preliminary LCI: leaks of chlorine, SO2, iodine and HCl need the component count of the P&IDs (out of scope of this run). Open gap for basic engineering, not negligible by evidence.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:melter-vent (The melter at about 393 K may release HCl and iodine vapour from the cake liquor; the balance sends them to the spent acid (D-006). Its vent and scrubber are not designed at concept stage. Upper bound for HCl: all of the cake's HCl (S17, 0.46 kg/h, 3.7 g per kg iodine, 0.4 % of the product mass) if none stayed in the acid. Iodine: not bounded, the vent gas flow is not designed (the 2026-10-28 review estimates iodine's vapour pressure near its melting point at the order of 10 kPa, not sourced here; the bound depends on that flow). The schema has no amount field for an exclusion, so the cut-off rule cannot be checked by cheme. Open gap.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:storage-and-unloading (Chlorine, SO2 and acid storage and unloading are outside the designed steps; their vents (to a caustic scrubber in practice) are not estimated. Open gap.)
