## Limitations

- gate-to-gate: no upstream supply chain
- construction (infrastructure, equipment) not included
- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:ammonia-slip (NH3 slip of the SCR: SRC-1 states a design limit of 10 ppmv at the end of the catalyst's life (Exhibit 5-5), not a time-average; the average slip is not sourced, so not estimated (D-011))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:startup (start-ups, shutdowns and part load (higher CO and NOx before the SCR is in its window) are not modelled: base load only (D-002))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:leaks (fugitive natural gas from the fuel skids and the plant's gas piping is not estimated at the concept stage; the pipeline's own losses belong to the natural gas background)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:blowdown-effluent (the blowdown's dissolved solids, treatment chemicals and biocide are not modelled; it goes to treatment (gap above))
