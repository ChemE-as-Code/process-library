## Boundary check

No warning from the boundary check, whose warnings are that:

- steps of the unit kinds compressor, pump, heater, cooler, shortcut_column, conversion_reactor, equilibrium_reactor, gibbs_reactor have an energy stream at a port (or are declared adiabatic) (warning when not)
- every product terminal has a category (warning when not)

It notes (info, not a gap):

- every feed terminal has a category
- energy terminals are classified utility; heat utilities declare a kind and supply temperature

It does not check:

- steps of other unit kinds (component separators, flash drums, mixers, splitters, unknown classes)
- whether a step's energy stream reaches a utility terminal (a step-to-step stream counts)
- whether duties are given or balance (cheme process energy)

- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included

- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:reactor-vents (R-101's vent (the isobutene and isobutane with the vapour past E-103) is the flowsheet's S8 to the oxidiser (product:OXID); R-102 forms no gas, and its filling and emptying, like R-101's, go through the nitrogen blanket to the same vent header; not estimated beyond S8 (no P&ID yet, D-008).)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:tank-breathing (Breathing and filling losses of the still feed and recycle tanks and of the still go through the nitrogen blanket to the vent header and the oxidiser; not estimated (no P&ID yet, D-014).)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:flaker-dust (TBSCl vapour and dust at the enclosed flaker under nitrogen and at packing (3.4 kPa for the subcooled liquid at 308 K on its ASSUMED vapour-pressure line, less over the solid, D-007) are not estimated; the enclosure's nitrogen goes to the vent header.)
