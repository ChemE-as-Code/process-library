## Route and basis
CO and phosgene-grade Cl₂ react over activated carbon in a multitubular reactor cooled by boiling water. The phosgene is condensed in two stages (cooling water to 308 K, then brine to 253 K). The vent goes through a caustic scrubber that destroys the leftover phosgene and Cl₂, then a thermal oxidiser burns the excess CO.

- **Capacity:** the 12.5 t/h of Cl₂ from chlor-alkali-1 becomes 17 346 kg/h of liquid phosgene (about 139 kt/a at 8000 h/a).
- **Yield and purity:** 99.47 % yield on chlorine, with 35 ppm(wt) Cl₂ in the product.
