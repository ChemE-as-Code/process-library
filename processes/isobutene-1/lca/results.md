- steps of the unit kinds compressor, pump, heater, cooler, shortcut_column, conversion_reactor, equilibrium_reactor, gibbs_reactor have an energy stream at a port (or are declared adiabatic) (warning when not)

- steps of other unit kinds (component separators, flash drums, mixers, splitters, unknown classes)

- whether duties are given or balance (cheme process energy)

- construction (infrastructure, equipment) not included
- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: cutoff:catalyst (catalyst charge of roughly 4-11 t (WHSV 1-3 1/h of US 8,633,345 B2 for 11.2 t/h feed) replaced every few years, about 3 t/a (ASSUMED order of magnitude, life not sourced; D-005, I-03); no catalyst inventory at this stage)
- cut off by the study's criteria: cutoff:hot-oil-and-fans (about 30 kW x 8000 h/a (ASSUMED order of magnitude, not sized; D-009); not in the flowsheet's power feed)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:heater-combustion (the fired heater's combustion emissions (CO2, NOx, CO, CH4, N2O, PM) belong to the purchased-heat background of feed:FUEL (heat from natural gas at an industrial furnace), which is per MJ delivered and includes them; counting them here would double count (D-011))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:storage-tanks (MTBE, isobutene and methanol tanks are outside the battery limit (design basis): their breathing and pressure-storage losses belong to the storage, not to this plant boundary (D-011))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:fugitive-leaks (fugitive isobutene, MTBE, methanol and DME from flanges, seals and valves are not estimated, not claimed negligible: an equipment-leak estimate needs the P&ID component counts, which do not exist at the concept stage (DESIGN.md I-05, D-011))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:vents-to-fuel (the DME-rich vents and the heavy ends leave the boundary as co-products to the site fuel systems (FUELGAS, HEAVIES); their combustion emissions belong to the user of the fuel, not to this plant (D-008, D-011))
