- steps of the unit kinds compressor, pump, heater, cooler, shortcut_column, conversion_reactor, equilibrium_reactor, gibbs_reactor have an energy stream at a port (or are declared adiabatic) (warning when not)

- steps of other unit kinds (component separators, flash drums, mixers, splitters, unknown classes)

- whether duties are given or balance (cheme process energy)

## Limitations

- gate-to-gate: no upstream supply chain
- construction (infrastructure, equipment) not included
- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:fugitive-leaks (Not estimated in this preliminary LCI: equipment leaks of HCN, acetone, methanol, MMA and SO2 need the component count of the P&IDs (out of scope at concept stage). An open gap, not negligible (HCN and ACH are acutely toxic).)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:furnace-nox-acid-mist (NOx of the SAR furnace (thermal NOx and the ammonium nitrogen of the spent acid, which the model sends entirely to N2) and sulfuric acid mist of the final absorber are not estimated: no sourced emission factor was read in this run. Open gap for the next stage.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:process-vents (Vents of the ACH reactor, the amidation, the esterification and the columns (to a scrubber or the SAR furnace in practice) are not in the flowsheet; amounts not known at concept stage. Open gap.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:storage (Acetone, methanol, ACH, MMA and acid tanks (breathing losses) are not designed at concept stage. Open gap.)
