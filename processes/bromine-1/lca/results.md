## Limitations

- gate-to-gate: no upstream supply chain
- construction (infrastructure, equipment) not included
- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:vent (process vent, not a leak: the separator and C-104 vent of non-condensables (air in-leakage, inerts of the chlorine, nitrogen from ammonia oxidation) with its bromine and chlorine traces, normally scrubbed, is not in the concept balance (the chlorine is taken inert-free, D-003); to be added with a vent scrubber at the next stage)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:h2s-ammonia (process vent, not a leak: the brine's H2S is taken fully oxidised to sulfur in C-101 and its ammonia (150-200 ppm, US 4 719 096) is not modelled; any H2S stripped overhead or degassed before the tower and the N2/chloramines from ammonia are not estimated)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:non-routine (start-up, shutdown and upsets (bromine or chlorine releases to the emergency scrubber) not estimated at the concept stage)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:tanks (bromine storage and loading are outside the boundary (D-001))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:leaks (fugitive Br2 and Cl2 from flanges and seals are not estimated at the concept stage; to be added with the P&IDs)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:effluent (no surface-water effluent: the tail brine is reinjected (treated as a waste to the reinjection route, product:TAILB) and the condensed live steam leaves with it)
