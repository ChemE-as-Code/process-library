## Engineering checks

- **Headers and tees.** The model's header segments end at tees
  T1041 127.057, T1052 219.653, T1063 221.653, T1074 223.653, T1085 241.705, T1091 243.705,
  T1115 245.705, T1130 291.0 and T1132 293.0 (supply), and T1042 127.057 through T1133 291.057
  (return). That is the P-114 order, with each tee placed where its user projects onto the header.
  The first branch starts exactly at each segment end, so core#95 is closed. Tees are 2 m apart
  centre to centre on a DN 1400 header; with branches up to DN 700 that leaves about 1.3 m clear
  between branch pipes, which can be built. The tee order sends CWS-1063 13 m and CWS-1074 18 m
  back west. CWS-1091 and CWR-1092 (K-501) each run about 62 m back west; this is already tracked
  as design-s11#2.
- **Buried cover.** The headers' top of pipe is 3.09 m below grade, including under R-5 (x 155)
  and R-6 (x 265), against `road_cover_m` 1.2. The shallowest horizontal buried legs are
  CWS-1091 (DN 450, 1.17 m), CWS-1063 (DN 400, 1.20 m) and CWS-1132; every one has at least
  1.0 m of cover. No branch crosses a road. The only horizontal CW legs above grade are the
  nozzle leads and the TV valve legs at the users (TV-1306 at 1.25 m, TV-1408 at 0.4-0.6 m,
  TV-1608 at 0.9 m). Removing the four headroom acceptances was correct: none of their ids
  matches a finding any more. No acceptance was added or widened, and no `skip:` was added.
- **Trays.** The fill of every power tray is at most 29 % (PT-PR-1-PR-10-AA) against the 30 %
  default. Every instrument tray is at most 39 %, under its default. The HV feeders run in
  PR-1 trays A/B, PR-10 and PR-7; the two 400 V MCC-1 feeders run in PR-1 tray C. Spot checks
  against the cited defaults in `cheme schema trays`: `fill_max_power` 0.3, `separation_m` 0.3
  (EN 50174-2 without a divider) and `lv_max_v` 1000 V (IEC 60038) all match D-048 and
  tray-rules.yaml. Downstream of PR-1 the split stops (finding #2).
- **Plan of the buried layer** (`cheme layout render --plan --z -5,0 --crop 115,30,305,195`):
  the two headers run along PR-1. The branch fan between x 205 and 250 is as the report gives
  it. CWS-1091 and CWR-1092 run south to y 43 and back west to K-501. Nothing runs where the
  report does not say it should. With column feet overlaid from the model, the conflicts in
  finding #1 show up.
- **D-047 support steel.** Unchanged against main. The S10 records reviewed it twice, and
  s10-r2#2 (the 252 vs 260 post count in D-047) is still open.
- **Provenance and sources.** Cover, depths, dead ends and tray widths are ASSUMED or named
  package defaults. No new external source. No third-party content found in the diff.
