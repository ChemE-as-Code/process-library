## The S5b items, checked

1. **maturity-balanced, partly done.** The model basis now passes: the 1.0 conversions of R100
   (C2H6/C3H8), R102A (O2), R401 (CO/CO2) and H101 (CH4, C2H6, C3H8, H2, NH3) are `given`, each
   with its source and locator. The values are D-005's, and they are physically right for
   these duties. Two of the H101 entries stretch the provenance (finding 1).
   `sourced.boundary` still fails. D-034's reason is factually right: S1, S12/S61 and S26/S70 are
   design results of this flowsheet (fuel from the radiant balance, process air from H2/N2 = 3,
   water from the water balance). A published source can give the gas composition and the
   specific consumption, but not these rates. The feed composition could still be sourced: the
   EBTF trial converged and was reverted only because the steam-system package then moved
   without explanation (finding 2). That leaves 2026-10-27-pfd-lci-review#3 open. Two routes
   remain, and neither may be taken by an agent alone. (i) The owner specifies the gas, air and
   water rates, as asu-1's owner did for its air. (ii) The project adopts a published reference
   plant's feed basis (composition and consumption per tonne, scaled to 1500 t/d) as its design
   basis, and the remaining feeds are then checked against it rather than given. Route (ii)
   still needs a core answer on how a feed that a constraint fixes is to be recorded.
2. **lifting-study, 26 -> 11 lanes, by design.** The 13 pads and 2 laydowns lie on open ground.
   The build has no clash, and the PG-1120 via removes the V-501 lane blocker. The E-504 move was
   tried and reverted for a real headroom clash. Nothing was accepted. Of the 11 lanes left,
   7 are blocked by the item's own connections (K-501, E-501, E-505, V-701, part of E-504) or
   by its own radiant-box tubes (H-101). These are standard "set the item before its piping"
   cases, which `build/lifting-acceptance-proposal.md` puts to the owner as the plan asks. The
   4 blocked by neighbouring piping (E-104, E-402, E-502, E-303/E-301) are also sequence
   questions. Two of them, E-301 (7.9 t) and E-303 (7.2 t), are light enough for a small crane
   at a short radius. The proposal itself suggests "a small crane from the north" for E-301,
   which a further pad could still test. No crane chart was produced: R-501 (658 t) and C-301
   (514 t) are stated to need a 1,000 t class crawler, but nothing checks that.
3. **Class 4 estimate, not started.** rates.yaml is the scaffold example. There is no CEPCI,
   no `cheme assess equipment` and no estimate. The TEA is unchanged.
4. **Other findings.** simulation#1 is closed by e324a09. Nothing else changed.

So 2026-10-29-design#1 stays open, with (b) reduced to 11 lanes and (c) and (d) unchanged in kind.
