## Engineering check

Recalculated by hand, independently of the script:

- Fire, V-201: 43 200 x 96.8^0.82 = 1.84 MW; 70 900 x 96.8^0.82 = 3.01 MW. Water at 40.9 bar(a):
  Tsat 525 K, latent heat about 1 705 kJ/kg, so 3 876 / 6 361 kg/h. Matches.
- Fire, V-701: relieving 26.5 x 1.21 + 1.013 = 33.1 bar(a). The NH3 saturation temperature
  (about 343 K) and latent heat (about 940 kJ/kg) are plausible, so 10 014 kg/h undrained.
  Matches.
- Tube rupture, E-502: S43 at 151 bar(a), 703 K, M 11.71, k 1.30. The choked mass flux is
  about 14 250 kg/(m2 s); through 3.45 cm2 that gives 17 725 kg/h. Matches.
- Blow-by, LV-1405: 27.3 x 2/3 x sqrt(0.685 x 31 x 17.9) = 355 kg/h per Kv. The
  rich-solvent Kv is 1 313 x sqrt(1.04/28.5) = 251, which gives 358 fully open and an
  indicative 127 000 kg/h. Matches. The LV-1903 numbers (Kv 7.2 / 10.2, 16 000 kg/h) match
  as well.
- Relief lines: I estimated the API 520 orifice for PSV-1212. It needs about 2 260 mm2 (an
  M orifice, normally a 4M6 valve with a DN 150 outlet), so the DN 100 PA-1054 is too small.
  This supports R-5.
- Set pressures equal the design pressures in `equipment/equipment-list.yaml` (K-101 38.5,
  V-201 33, C-302 2.5, K-501 159.5, V-501 161, V-502 26.5, V-701 26.5, U-801 49.5 barg).
- R-1 is sound: on a K-501 stop the PSV-1310 relieving pressure of 36.3 barg exceeds 1.1 x 31
  barg (V-401). The PSV-1704 loop claim holds: the make-up enters at the K-502 suction, and
  V-501 sits about 1 bar above the suction. At the PSV-1704 relieving pressure (175.5 barg),
  V-501 therefore stays just under 1.1 x 161 = 177.1 barg. The margin is thin but positive.
- Cited values spot-checked against general engineering knowledge of the cited standards:
  - API 521 wetted-wall fire: SI coefficients 43 200 / 70 900, exponent 0.82, 7.6 m.
  - IEC 60534-2-1 choked gas: N6 = 27.3 (kg/h, bar, kg/m3), Y = 2/3, x = F_gamma xT.
  - Accumulation 10 % / 21 %.

  All are the standard published values. The study says honestly that the standards
  themselves were not read.

## Verdict

Approve. No critical or high finding is open. The section target is met:
- every drawn relief device has its cases and a named governing case;
- required capacity is quantified for five devices;
- for the other three the missing input is stated (control valve sizes, a supercritical fire
  method). Core has no control-valve, relief-orifice or supercritical-fire method, as the plan
  allows;
- the gate `relief-on-pid` passes.

The study's own design findings R-1 to R-5 (unprotected V-401 train on a K-501 stop,
hydrogen-rich atmospheric discharge, E-502 tube rupture into steam, relief lines and flare
header too small) are real. They are tracked to open HAZOP actions (A16/A17, A22, A45, A34)
for the next P&ID revision and the flare study. They are not defects of this section.
