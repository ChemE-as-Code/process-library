## Round 1 findings

- **s10#1 (high), PC-1083 through V-401's ladder: closed.** The pin now runs along y 128 at 0.35 m
  (0.583 m out of the bottom nozzle to x 233.5). It jogs to y 127 only at x 249, 16 m east of the
  ladder. Its rails stand at y 126.59-127.00, so the pipe is 1.0 m north of them. SLP-V401-00
  (width 0.6 m about y 128) ends 0.7 m from the rails. The `layout-ladder-clearance` instance is
  gone (9 -> 8) and nothing new appears at V-401, E-401, LD-202 or CP-204.
- **s10#2 (medium), low-point drains only in prose: closed.** It was closed by the first fix the
  record offered. The DW-1073 drain is drawn on P-106: tee DW1073_LPD downstream of FV-1508, and a
  DN 25 ball valve on segment S3. The topology agrees with the 3D route, where the tee sits at the
  foot of the drop at (229.0, 163.97). The drains of PC-1083 and PC-1084 and the CO2-1142 drain
  pot are in the line-list notes with their sheet, ASSUMED, "to be drawn". D-047 states the
  pot's drainage (float trap, closed, to U-801's condensate collection), the 1:100 fall to the
  pot and the winterisation (tracing and insulation). The pot is at the downstream riser (y 196.2),
  which matches the gas direction of the pinned route. One detail of the drawn drain is
  s10r2#3.
- **s10#3 (low), K-501 tray in the lane: closed.** The cable entry offset is now 4.5 m, so the drop
  comes off PR-7 between the tier-8 beams. PT-M-K-501 is no longer a lane blocker.
- **s10#4 (low), sleepers and crossings unrecorded: closed.** The 26 HEB100 sleepers are in the
  model and the take-off: 0.574 t over 28.12 m is 20.4 kg/m, the EN 10365 HEB100 mass. Their tops
  fit the pipes: CO2-1142 DN 65 has its bottom at 0.312 m, less 0.1 m for the shoe, giving 0.21 m.
  The shared sleepers span x 228.43-229.63 and carry all three lines (x 228.6 / 229.0 / 229.45).
  D-047 lists three step-over locations (ASSUMED) as an allowance outside the take-off.
- **s10#5 (low), review gates stale: closed.** PROGRESS.md states that `design-review` stays stale
  until the next design review. The PR body still says "Gates: unchanged", which this record and
  the PR comment correct.
