## Engineering checks

- **Removing the headroom acceptances was correct.** The four removed ids
  (`layout-headroom:P-114/Line_CWR1145_S2+R-5`, `…_S8+R-6`, `…CWS1144_S2+R-5`, `…_S8+R-6`) no
  longer match any finding: the build reports no headroom finding at all. The crossings are now
  checked for soil cover instead. The DN 1400 headers have 3.09 m over the top under R-5 and R-6,
  more than the 1.2 m `road_cover_m`, and there is no `layout-buried-cover` finding. The owner's
  condition (sleeve or slab under the road, I-006/D-041) stays in the `buried.basis` of both
  headers and in D-048 item 1. The checks.yaml diff only removes those four entries and adds a
  comment. No acceptance was added or widened. No `skip:` was added in routing.yaml, and the 8
  lifting acceptances still each match a current finding.
- **Depth arithmetic in D-048 item 3 checks out.** For the DN 1400 header (OD 1.422 m) at
  3.8 m, the top is 3.089 m down. For a DN 700 branch at 2.4 m, the bottom is 2.756 m, 0.33 m
  above the header top. A DN 700 hop at 1.4 m keeps 1.04 m cover. Across the model, no buried
  CW leg runs horizontally above −1.0 m. The only legs above that are the risers, TV valve legs
  and nozzle leads at the users. The shallowest buried legs are the 1.4 m hops of CWS-1063
  (DN 400), CWS-1091 (DN 450) and CWS-1132 (DN 125), each with ≥ 1.17 m cover. This closes
  detail-gates#2.
- **Tray split (spot check against the electrical list and the schedule).** SWG-1 is 10 kV and
  MCC-1 is 400 V (electrical-list.yaml:16-17). The schedule has 9 cables from SWG-1 (K-101,
  K-102, K-103, K-501, K-502, K-701, P-301A/B, P-801) and 2 from MCC-1 (P-302A/B), as D-048
  item 4 states. The 11 power cable lengths add up to 3,339 m, as stated. Fill on PT-PR-1-A-1 is
  26 %, under the 30 % default. The voltage-class split is a normal arrangement. Widths and fill
  are package defaults, marked ASSUMED in tray-rules.yaml.
- **Road cover (spot check against the cited default).** `cheme schema routing` gives a
  `road_cover_m` default of 1.2, described as ASSUMED, as D-048 cites it. `cover_m` 1.0 is
  ASSUMED with its reason: no frost or traffic data, open input I-03.
- **Estimate and MTO movement.** Pipe went from 12,609.9 to 12,638.2 m (+28.3 m). The CW lines
  are now shown as their own `buried` rows: 1,803.9 m, of which 493 m is DN 1400. Elbows went
  from 1,453 to 1,401, because router routes have fewer bends than the hand pins. The estimate
  rose by 14.25 (103,353.55 to 103,367.80). The example rates price all pipe at 2 per metre
  whatever the DN, so the movement says nothing about cost. Buried pipe is priced like
  above-grade pipe, and earthworks are missing (see design-s11#1). D-048 discloses both.
- **D-047 (S10)** was reviewed twice for layout. As a design decision: about 117 t of IPE240
  intermediate steel at 2 m on the small-bore tiers is the right order. That is roughly 3.8 km
  of beam over PR-1, PR-2, PR-4, PR-7 and PR-8. The 2.1 m span used for DN 25 liquid is a usual
  value. Rejecting intermediate steel on every tier is reasoned. The drains of PC-1083, PC-1084
  and CO2-1142 are tracked in the line-list notes as ASSUMED, still to be drawn. The 252 to 260
  post-count correction is stated in D-048 but not in D-047 (s10-r2#2, carried).
- **DW-1073 blind flange** (P-106, s10-r2#3): a BlindFlange DN 25 with a DirectPipingConnection
  from the ball-valve outlet. P-106 validates and its drawing counts are clean. Closed.
- **Provenance.** Every new number in D-048 and the YAML is marked ASSUMED (cover, depths, dead
  ends, tray widths and fill) or is a package default named as such. No new external source.
  No third-party content found in the diff.
- **DESIGN.md and PROGRESS.md say what was done**, including the core-defect episode and the
  v1.2.1 rebuild. One gap: the core v1.2.0 rebuild also moved some non-CW routes, which neither
  file mentions (design-s11#3).
