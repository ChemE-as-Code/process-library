## Engineering spot checks

I recalculated these values myself; all agree with the line list:
- **Refrigerant to E-504:** Q25 22.5 MW over (h_fg 258 K minus the liquid sensible heat from
  313 K) gives about 21.3 kg/s, against 21.4 kg/s in the line list.
- **AG-1118:** at 236 kPa(a) and 258 K the vapour density is 1.87 kg/m3. That gives 11.4 m3/s
  and d = 0.85 m at 20 m/s, so DN 900.
- **AL-1117:** DN 200.
- **AG-1122:** 23.2 kg/s at 1555 kPa(a) and 380 K gives d = 0.42 m, so DN 450.
- **CW headers:** 136.5 MW is the sum Q2+Q16+Q18+Q19+Q21+Q24+Q28+Q33. At a 10 K rise that is
  3266 kg/s, and d = 1.37 m at 2.2 m/s, so DN 1400.
- **SM-1140:** 42.6 kg/s at about 15.6 kg/m3 gives d = 0.34 m, so DN 350.
- **SL-1135:** 0.18 kg/s at 450 kPa(a) gives DN 65.
- **AL-1128:** 62.5 t/h of liquid NH3 at 239 K gives DN 150.

I spot-checked two cited values against their sources:
- **NH3 saturation pressures:** 236 kPa(a) at 258 K and 1555 kPa(a) at 313 K (thermo, D-029)
  match standard NH3 saturation data.
- **D-015:** it does name an LP let-down for the HRU without giving a pressure (0.4 t/h), so the
  ASSUMED 450 kPa(a) is stated honestly.

The E-502 steam rate (Q23 43.2 MW / 25.9 kg/s = 1669 kJ/kg) matches the water heat of
vaporisation at 4.6 MPa.

On provenance, the new DNs and package boundaries are marked ASSUMED. No flowsheet value was
edited by hand: the flowsheet diff only adds `links`. No gate was relaxed or accepted. I found no
third-party content.
