## Engineering checks

- **Pads at grade.** I re-ran `build/review_pads.py` (all 31 pads and 14 laydowns against
  items, structures, racks, roads, hazardous zones, pipes below 4 m and each other). It finds no
  overlap. DW-1073 is off CP-203.
- **Pads under piping.** I re-ran `build/review_pads_overhead.py` and the branch's
  `cheme run scripts/pad_search.py overhead`. Both agree:
  - CP-204, CP-205, CP-206, CP-207, CP-212 and the new CP-213 have no pipe over them in plan.
  - Two pads still do: CP-211 (E-301) has AM-1059 at 4.75 m just inside its west edge, and
    CP-703 (V-701) has AG-1121 at 4.8 m through its middle and AL-1114/AL-1127 at 9 m.
  - D-033 states this.
- **What the two remaining pads mean.** I tested shifted pads with a scratch script
  (`build/review_r3_pads.py`, which uses the core lane rule).
  - Moving CP-211 1 to 1.5 m east, clear of AM-1059, makes the core report **AM-1059 as a lane
    blocker** for E-301.
  - For V-701, every pad clear of pipes is blocked by AL-1128: west (307-315, 66-74), north
    (316-324, 74-82), east (327-335, 66-74) and the old CP-702.
  - So E-301 and V-701 show `lane clear` only because the blocking pipe lies over the pad
    footprint, which the core rule does not check. In substance they are blocked lanes too. The
    study's real count is 10, not 8.
  - The owner proposal (`build/lifting-acceptance-proposal.md`) still counts both among the 18
    lanes "cleared by design" and does not list them. This is finding 1.
- **DW-1073.** The pinned route matches the commit text: x = 229 at 0.6 m from y 163.97 to
  195.5, up to 8.0 m, west to x = 209.5, then down to V-302.
  - The vehicle access from R-2 (y = 210) to CP-211, CP-212 and X-401 is now under an 8 m
    pipe. The CP-203 access from the north does not cross x = 229. layout-s5b-r2#1 is closed.
  - The re-pin makes a 7.4 m deep U-pocket in a DN 25 line, with two free-standing risers at
    x = 229 in open ground with no steel near them. The low point needs a drain. The 31 m grade
    leg lies 1 m from X-401's east face, in the 2 m strip between X-401 and its crane pad. This
    is finding 3 (low). The route is now 404 m long, which adds to layout#8.
- **E-504.** The proposal row now explains why the 2 m move is not offered: it was tried and
  gave a PG-1107 headroom clash, and E-504's own refrigerant lines would still cross the lane.
  layout-s5b-r2#3 is closed.
- **The 8 listed lanes.** The blocking objects match `cheme layout lifting`. The reasons are in
  our own words and plausible (own piping, adjacent exchanger piping, H-101 radiant box). None
  is in an accepted list.
- **The proposal's crane paragraph** still says a load chart "may not be copied into this
  CC BY 4.0 library". D-033 withdrew that reason in r2 (design-s5b-r2#2): a few
  capacity-at-radius values with a source could be cited. This is finding 2 (low).
- **Spot-checks of cited values** (process/reactions.yaml, D-034):
  - **EP 1586535 A2.** Checked on Google Patents. The applicant is Air Products and Chemicals
    and the publication date is 2005-10-19. The background says a prereformer converts
    substantially all of the higher hydrocarbons in natural gas to methane, carbon oxides and
    hydrogen. **Confirmed.** The page shows no paragraph numbers, so "[0003]" was not verified.
  - **Al-Dhfeery and Jassem (2012), Int. J. Ind. Chem. 3:14.** Checked in the open-access PDF
    (Strathprints). Page 2 has the combustion-zone model, written "with the assumption that ...
    complete oxygen consumption is considered", and H2 burning to H2O. Table 2 compares the
    model output with plant data. **Confirmed** as cited.
  - Note: in the paper, complete O2 consumption is a model assumption that the plant
    comparison supports, not a measurement. `given` for R102A is defensible on that basis. This
    is design scope and not raised as a finding here.
- **Third-party content.** D-033, the routing comments and the proposal are in our own words.
