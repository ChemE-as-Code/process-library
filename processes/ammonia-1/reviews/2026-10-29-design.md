## Engineering plausibility (design case, from `cheme process streams`)

- Steam to carbon 3.0 at the prereformer (S7); primary reformer outlet 1083 K, CH4 12.0 mol%
  dry; secondary outlet 1265 K, CH4 0.4 mol% dry; HTS exit CO 3.6 mol% dry, LTS exit 0.38 mol%
  dry; treated gas 496 ppm CO2. All are in the usual range for an air-blown SMR ammonia front end.
- Radiant duty 68.6 MW against about 122 MW (LHV) fired (natural gas S2 plus tail gas S59), a
  radiant efficiency of about 56 %. Flue O2 1.7 mol% wet. Plausible.
- Carbon balance: the process gas carbon (1739 kmol/h) leaves as vented CO2 (1658 kmol/h, S28),
  as carbon in the treated gas (71 kmol/h, methanated to the purge) and as CO2 in the condensate
  (7 kmol/h). It closes. Vented process CO2 is 1.17 t per t NH3.
- Converter: NH3 3.34 to 18.44 mol% at 151 bar, 12.1 % inerts, 494 to 703 K. That is about 14 K
  per % NH3. The outlet reaction quotient (about 0.0083 atm^-1) sits below the ideal-gas Kp at
  703 K once a fugacity correction is applied, which is consistent with the 15 K approach of the
  flowsheet.
- Machines: K-501 12.7 MW against 9.0 MW isothermal (28.5 to 146 bar), K-101 plus K-101D 8.6 MW
  against 5.5 MW isothermal, refrigeration COP 2.2. Plausible.
- Natural gas 27.9 GJ(LHV)/t NH3 (feed 21.8, fuel 6.1), plus 36.6 MW of electricity for the
  electric drives (D-016) and 60.1 t/h of MP steam exported. A plausible total for a modern
  plant.
- Steam: 130 MW of waste heat at 3.03 GJ/t raises about 155 t/h. Process steam 92.8, export 60.1
  and blowdown 3.1 t/h; the water balance closes with 105 t/h DMW.
