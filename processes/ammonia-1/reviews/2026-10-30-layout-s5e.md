## Engineering checks

- **Tray ends against the lanes.** In the model:
  - PT-M-K-101 ends at (135, 185, 4.5): 3 m east of the casing, below the 5 m machine top.
  - PT-M-K-501 ends at 5.5 m, north of the machine (top 6 m, terminal-box top 5.75 m).
  - PT-M-K-701 ends at 4.5 m, north of the machine (top 5 m).
  - PT-M-K-102 and PT-M-K-103 end at 3.46 m, beside their fans.

  So the lanes pass by geometry, not by an acceptance.
- **Spot-checks against what D-046 cites.**
  - The K-501 terminal box in the model is centred at 5.426 m and 0.653 m high, so its top is
    at 5.75 m. The list height is 6.0 m (`equipment-list.yaml:59`). Both match D-046.
  - The K-701 terminal-box top is at 4.795 m, against a 5.0 m list height
    (`equipment-list.yaml:70`). This matches D-046 too.
- **Motor-removal envelopes.** I compared each tray end with the `motor_removal` envelope that
  the build generates (finding #1):
  - K-102, K-501 and K-701 are clear.
  - K-101 and K-103 are not. Their tray ends sit directly above the envelope, because the "east
    side" chosen for them is the motor's withdrawal end.
- **Crane pads above and below grade.** I wrote the scratch script
  `build/review_s5e_pads.py`. It lists every pipe, tray and duct over (z > 0.3 m) or under
  (z < 0) each crane pad in plan:
  - Nothing runs over or under the new CP-507, as D-046 says.
  - CP-211 and CP-703 still have pipes overhead (layout-s5b-r3#1).
  - Six pads lie over buried cooling-water lines, CP-505 among them (finding #2).
- **CP-507 in plan** (render `--plan --crop 205,45,260,100`):
  - It lies 1 m south of the E-502 shell and 1 m north of PR-7, and it overlaps neither.
  - E-502, E-504 and E-505 lift from it at 11-13 m radius.
  - Pipes rising from E-502 and E-504 to PR-7 fence it in on the west and east. A crane reaches
    it only under PR-7 (lowest tier 6 m) or between those risers. D-046 states this as ASSUMED,
    and design-s5e#3 carries the pad size and the governing lift.
- **E-503 cooling-water leads.**
  - CWS-1115 and CWR-1116 leave S1 and S2 at 0.75 m and go down at y 81.79 to the buried
    branches at -1.8 m. They no longer cross E-505's lane.
  - The CWR riser is 0.33 m from the CP-505 edge (pipe OD to the pad box).
  - The shell-venting question this raises is design-s5e#1.
- **Quantities.** The regenerated MTO and estimate are current. The D-046 deltas (DN 350
  -8.2 m, bulk total -0.07 %) match the files (see design-s5e).
- **Scope and sources.**
  - Only S5e files changed.
  - Every new position, side, clearance and pad is marked ASSUMED, with the reason in D-046.
  - No third-party content is added.
