## The fixes, checked

- **Cooling water price (product:CW, -2.85 EUR/MWh).** The arithmetic was recomputed and is
  right. Circulation is 3600 MJ / (4.18 kJ/kg K x 10 K) = 86.1 t/MWh. The pump at 40 m and 0.75
  takes 45.1 MJ = 0.01252 MWh. The fans at 150 Pa and 0.65 take 0.00476 MWh, with an air mass
  equal to the water mass (L/G about 1) at 1.16 kg/m3. Together that is 1.74 EUR at 100.65
  EUR/MWh. Make-up is 1.481 t evaporated x 5/4 = 1.852 t at 0.60 EUR/t, which is 1.11 EUR. The
  two property values were spot-checked: the latent heat of water at 30 °C is 2430 kJ/kg (steam
  tables, about 2429.8), and air at 303 K and 1 atm is 101325/(287 x 303) = 1.165 kg/m3. Both
  are confirmed. The cooling tower is outside the boundary (D-001), so its pump power is not
  also in the plant's 36.6 MW. There is no double count. 136.5 MW goes to cooling water (tea.md),
  which is plausible for an all-electric plant with the refrigeration condenser and the
  compressor intercoolers. All values are ASSUMED, with the premises written in the source.
- **DMW 2.0 EUR/t** is a stated screening value, ASSUMED, with no quote and a swing of 0.5-2.0.
  It is honest. The rate is 106.6 t/h. **Blowdown `free`** has a reason (to the cooling-tower
  make-up outside the boundary, ASSUMED). This is common practice. The LCOP goes from 738.6 to
  748.2 EUR/t. Back-calculated, 9.6 EUR/t x 62.5 t/h equals 213 EUR/h for DMW plus 389 EUR/h for
  CW, which agrees with tea.md. The gate `tea` passes on the project's own premises, and nothing
  was relaxed.
- **Weights.** The script now uses the higher of the notes and the linked streams' design
  pressures. Check for E-501 at 15,500 kPa(a), 1.8 m x 12 m: the wall is about 120 mm and the
  shell about 64 t, which the internals factor raises to the listed 167.5 t. R-100 at about
  4.2 MPa, 4.5 m x 10 m: the wall is about 76 mm, giving 120.9 t. Both are consistent with the
  stated method. Exchangers whose high-pressure stream is in the tubes (E-502, E-504) are now
  overstated rather than understated. For a lifting screen that is the conservative side, and it
  follows the action round 1 proposed. It is not a new finding.
- **D-030 open list** names the R-501 bed high-high trip and the K-502/K-701 trips, the V-502 to
  V-701 blow-by question (LL trip, or PSV-2010 sized for the full LV-1907 gas capacity), and the
  V-201 blow-by question. It names each with its hazard and the layers that exist. This is one of
  the two actions round 1 offered for each finding, and the HAZOP (S6) will pick them up.
- **Layout fixes in design-scope files**: PR-11 is in layout/site-plan.yaml:40. CP-204 gives
  E-401 a pad (lifting-study.md:32, lane still blocked by its own PC-1083/PG-1079). The rebuilt
  model has no zone finding. The H-101 fuel and tail-gas trains are still unzoned (package
  scope, stated in D-031).
