## Engineering spot checks

- IPE240 take-off: 117.065 t over 3813.2 m is 30.7 kg/m, the IPE240 mass per metre. Right.
- HEB200 at 61.3 kg/m (D-047): right for EN 10365.
- Suggested spans quoted in D-047 against ASME B31.1 Table 121.5: DN 25 water 7 ft (2.1 m), DN 80
  gas 15 ft (4.6 m), DN 300 gas 30 ft (9.1 m), DN 400 water 27 ft (8.2 m). All match.
- IPE240 screening deflection: a 10 m simply supported stringer with four 5 kN loads is about a
  2 kN/m uniform load. With EI = 210 GPa x 3892 cm4, 5wL^4/384EI is about 32 mm, or L/310. This
  matches the L/300 in D-047. It is ASSUMED and left to the structural design, as it should be.
- Headroom: the new stringers sit at 7.76 m or higher over roads R-5 and R-6 (PR-7 tier 8), above
  the 6 m headroom. PR-4 tier 6 was left out on purpose because of R-6. The 4 headroom findings
  are the same as on main.
- D-047 is in our own words. It cites the B31.1 table as the basis of core's ASSUMED table and
  copies none of it beyond the values used. Its reasons and ASSUMED values (section, spacing,
  sleeper height, drains) are stated.
- The core routing issue (a split line loses its rack) is reported, not worked around.
