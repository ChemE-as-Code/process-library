## Engineering checks

- **Pads and laydowns at grade.** I re-ran last round's scratch check (`build/review_pads.py`,
  run with `cheme run`). It overlays the 30 pads and 14 laydowns on items, structures, racks,
  roads, hazardous zones, pipes below 4 m and each other. LD-204 is now clear of R-5
  (layout-s5b#2 closed). The one overlap is new: **DW-1073 runs at 0.6 m across crane pad
  CP-203** (x = 230.5 m, y 164 to 195.5; CP-203 is x 230-240, y 178-188). This is X-401's pad.
  D-033 calls the run "sleeper height", but there is no sleeper way. It is 52 m of DN 25 pipe
  at 0.6 m in the open area of A-200: north along x = 230.5 m, then west along y = 195.5 m to
  V-302. It lies between X-401, the new pads CP-211/CP-212 and laydown LD-201 on one side and
  the north road R-2 on the other. A transport or crane crossing needs a pipe bridge or a
  sleeper crossing, and none is declared. This is finding 1.
- **Pipes over the pads.** The lifting rule checks only the lane between a pad and its item.
  Its own "Not covered" list names "the crane's own clearance to overhead lines and
  structures". I wrote a second scratch check (`build/review_pads_overhead.py`) that lists every
  pipe segment over each pad in plan, with its elevation:

  | pad | pipe over it (elevation) | where |
  |---|---|---|
  | CP-703 (V-701, new) | AG-1121 (4.8 m), AL-1114 (9.05 m), AL-1127 (8.89 m) | AG-1121 runs north-south through the middle of the pad at x = 320; the other two lie 1.9 m and 2 m in from its west and east edges |
  | CP-206 (S5b) | PG-1077 (4.75 m) | through the middle, x = 215 |
  | CP-205 (S5b) | PG-1033 (6.3 m) | x = 171.1, 6 m in from the west edge |
  | CP-207 (S5b) | BW-1039 (9.13 m), SM-1040 (9.01 m) | x = 175.7 and 179, both inside the pad |
  | CP-204 (S5) | PG-1080 (5.82 m) | along the north edge, y = 125.5 |
  | CP-211 (new) | AM-1059 (4.75 m) | 0.4 m inside the west edge |
  | CP-212 (new) | PG-1080 (4.82 m) | 0.3 m inside the east edge |

  A mobile crane cannot be rigged, luffed or slewed under a pipe 4.75 to 9 m up. So pads with a
  pipe across their middle are not usable crane positions, and the `lane clear` the study
  reports from them (V-701, and the items lifted from CP-205/206/207) is not demonstrated. The
  edge cases (CP-204, CP-211, CP-212) can be fixed by moving the pad half a metre. This is
  finding 2. The other 22 of the 30 pads have no pipe over them. CP-203 has only
  DW-1073 at grade (finding 1).
- **AL-1129 route.** It goes along PR-7 at 8.0 m to x = 326 m, up to 8.85 m, north to
  y = 71.7, down to 0.63 m and 6 m west at grade to T1129 beside V-701. It no longer crosses
  the drum's crown, and nothing at grade is in CP-703. The free 8 m drop at x = 326 m needs a
  support (generic free-span proposal, detail). The new route itself is acceptable. But the
  pad it was made for is crossed by AG-1121 (finding 2).
- **DW-1073 route.** The run over E-301/E-302/E-303 is gone, and the E-301 and E-303 lanes
  are clear (layout-s5b#1 closed for the routing part). The route is now about 380 m from the
  battery limit, including the vertical runs, with several unnecessary up-and-down steps (10.0 -> 10.85 -> 9.0 -> 9.85 ->
  8.0 m) where it changes rack. That is the layout#8 kind, carried. The grade run is
  finding 1.
- **The 8 lanes left.** I read the proposal against the warnings. The blocking objects listed
  match `cheme layout lifting`. The reasons are in our own words and are plausible for the
  item's own piping (K-501, E-501, E-505), the radiant box (H-101), and adjacent piping
  (E-104, E-402, E-502). The **E-504** row offers a layout fix: move E-504 2 m north and re-pin
  PG-1107 at the V-501 platform. It gives that fix to "detail", while the proposal's own text
  says every case is "not a layout change", and the plan asks to relocate items where the plot
  plan allows. This is finding 3 (low). Even moved, E-504 would still have its own refrigerant
  piping in the lane, so the lane would stay an erection-sequence case. That is why it is low.
- **Weights in the lifting table.** V-701 6.6 t and X-401 30 t (lift_weight) match the
  equipment list.
- **Spot-checks of cited values.** The layout files cite no published value. Pads, laydowns,
  criteria and routes are all marked ASSUMED, which is honest. I checked two values cited
  elsewhere on the branch against the published values as I know them, without re-downloading
  the sources. CEPCI annual 2023 = 797.9 (estimate/cost-basis.yaml) and the Turton
  vertical-vessel constants K1 3.4974, K2 0.4485, K3 0.1074 both agree. design-s5b-r2 checked
  the CEPCI PDF directly.
- **Third-party content.** D-033 and the proposal are in our own words. The crane-chart
  question is design-s5b-r2#2, carried.
