## Engineering assessment

- **Coverage.** Every CO2R and MET step and K-501 of SYN is on a sheet. The solvent-loop items
  are not PFD steps because D-009 lumps the loop into C301. They were added as a generic amine
  loop with ASSUMED sizes, which is honest. Off-page texts match on both sides for PG-1035,
  PG-1043, PG-1047, AM-1049, AM-1050, AM-1051, AM-1057, AM-1058, PG-1056 and PG-1081.
  Connectors to S3c (U-801, X-601, M502, flare, CW) are named and wait for S3c.
- **Line sizes.** I recomputed them from the stream table (`cheme process streams`):
  - S28 is 74,528 kg/h at x_CO2 0.9497, which gives 72.95 t/h CO2. At 18 m3/t that is 1,313
    m3/h, so DN 600 runs at 1.3 m/s and DN 500 at 1.9 m/s. Correct.
  - CO2-1068: 1,745 kmol/h at 150 kPa(a) and 313 K is 8.4 m3/s, or 13 m/s in DN 900. Correct.
  - PG-1056: about 7,600 kmol/h at 3,100 kPa(a) and 313 K gives d = 0.34 m at 20 m/s, so DN
    350. Correct.
  - PG-1088: 14,600 kPa(a) gives 0.38 m3/s, 12 m/s in DN 200. Correct.
  - Overhead CO2-1066 at a 1:1 water/CO2 ratio and 15 m/s gives d of about 1.2 m. Matches the
    ASSUMED DN 1200.
  - DW-1073: 1,488 kg/h gives DN 20 at 1.5 m/s. DN 25 is the stated minimum.
  - CW, 10 K rise: Q18/2 = 10.95 MW is 2.1 m/s in DN 400; Q19 17.7 MW is 2.2 m/s in DN 500;
    Q21 12.9 MW is 1.9 m/s in DN 450. These agree with the stated "up to 2.2 m/s".
- **Spot-check of cited values.** The section brings no new external source. I checked two of
  the values it cites against `process/flowsheet.yaml`. 72.95 t/h CO2 matches S28 (calculated,
  DWSIM). Q18 21.9 MW (ASSUMED) and Q21 12.9 MW (calculated) match the stream table.
- **Provenance.** Every new size, position, DN without a stream behind it, the E-302/E-303 split
  and the plate type of E-301 is marked ASSUMED in the lists and in D-028. The flowsheet edit
  is links only, with no value changed.
- **Control and trips.** These are sensible for a first revision:
  - LIC-1405 on the rich solvent has an independent LZ-1406/XV-1406 blow-by trip, I-105. This
    addresses the gap that pids-front#3 raised for V-201.
  - Lean-solvent flow and temperature are controlled, and so are the regenerator pressure on
    the vent, the reflux drum level and the make-up water.
  - The R-401 high-high temperature trip (I-106) has its own TZ sensor, separate from TI-1606.
  - The K-501 anti-surge recycle is taken after the aftercooler and upstream of the discharge
    check valve. It returns upstream of the UIC-1703 flow measurement.
  - PSV-1704 sits upstream of the discharge check valve.
  - All trips stay proposed for S6.
- **Licences.** The sheets are drawn by our own scripts and cheme catalogue shapes. No
  third-party content was found.
- **Scope.** Kept to S3b. The edits to P-103 and P-104 are off-page texts only. The rebuilt
  layout model is generated.
- **PR body.** It matches what I found, except the claim that signal lines no longer run along
  pipes (#4).
