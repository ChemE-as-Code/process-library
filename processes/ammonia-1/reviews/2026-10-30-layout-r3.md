## Engineering checks

- **layout-r2#1 (pockets in the PC-1048/PC-1084 pins).** I read the coordinates in
  routing.yaml:57-59. PC-1048 now goes 10.07 -> 9.07 -> 8.07 -> 7.93 -> 5.5 m and never rises.
  PC-1084 goes 8.017 -> 6.5 -> 5.5 m and never rises. The model reports no clash. **Closed
  2d93219.**
- **layout#4 remainder (H-101 fuel and tail-gas trains).** Z-HAZ-FUEL puts two 1 m zone-2
  spheres at the burner inlet nozzles: H-101/B2 at (61.5, 153.0, 22.19) and H-101/B1 at (53.6,
  153.0, 22.22) (plant-model.yaml:62611-62637). Its basis and D-031 say that "the nozzles stand
  for the valve trains until the burner-management package places them". But the model already
  places the valves it names:
  - PV-1101 at (69.04, 144.91, 8.04) (plant-model.yaml:68466-68472). XV-1102 is on the same
    segment and shares the valve access platform VP-PV-1101 at 68-71, 145-146, deck 7.28 m
    (plant-model.yaml:28605-28618).
  - XV-1105 at (129.90, 143.73, 8.07) (plant-model.yaml:68615-68621).

  PV-1101 is about 18 m from the B2 sphere and XV-1105 about 77 m from the B1 sphere. With a
  1 m radius, neither valve and none of their flanges is inside the zone, nor is the platform
  where people operate them. So the zone does not classify the releases it is declared for. The
  stated reason, that the trains are not placed, does not match the model. The schema also
  allows explicit volumes (`shape`/`centre`/`box`, `cheme schema zones`), so a zone at the valve
  points can be expressed even though a line source needs an open end. This is new finding
  layout-r3#1. layout#4 is closed as superseded by it.
- **Tail-gas substance.** The B1 source is declared `substance: hydrogen`. I checked S59 (tail
  gas to reformer fuel) in the stream table: CH4 0.535, H2 0.092, N2 0.210, Ar 0.161 (mole
  fractions, calculated). The gas is mostly methane. Declaring it as hydrogen is conservative
  for the gas group and the basis text says "hydrogen- and methane-bearing", so this is not a
  finding.
- **Lifting, estimate, maturity (design#1).** The lifting study shows the same 26 lanes. There
  is still no crane chart, no accepted lane, no estimate.csv, and no sourced feed or conversion
  basis. The PROGRESS response explains why the fix job cannot close these without accepting a
  gate or inventing data, and I agree. The finding stays open, high, and goes to the
  orchestrator or user under the runs/plan.md stop rules.
- **Sources spot-checked.**
  1. Tail-gas composition behind the Z-HAZ-FUEL basis ("hydrogen- and methane-bearing"): S59
     has CH4 0.535 and H2 0.092. Consistent.
  2. Fuel natural gas behind FG-1002: S2 has CH4 0.91, C2H6 0.04, C3H8 0.01, CO2 0.01, N2 0.03,
     which is a flammable gas of class IIA. The zone-2 grade for flanges and valves is
     consistent with that. The 1 m radius is the package's ASSUMED table and is marked ASSUMED.
- **No third-party content** in the changed files. Zones, routes and generated tables come from
  the project's own YAML.
