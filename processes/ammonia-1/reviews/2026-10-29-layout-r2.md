## Engineering checks

- **round 1 layout#1 (E-401 pad).** CP-204 gives E-401 a pad: 22.0 m radius, 34.7 t gross
  (lifting-study.md). V-401 moved to CP-204 and its lane is now clear. E-401, E-402 and E-203 have
  lanes blocked by their own PC-1083/PG-1079 piping. D-031 says 14 pads and 12 laydowns; zones.yaml
  has 14 `crane_pad` and 12 `laydown` entries. **Closed.**
- **round 1 layout#2 (east connector).** PR-11 exists and is used. AL-1128 is 432 -> 77 m, and the
  total pipe is 10,875 -> 10,687 m. BW-1100 still runs 331 m because its end is not on the east
  side, which D-031 states. **Closed.** Side effect: V-701's lane was clear in round 1. It is now
  blocked by AL-1128/AL-1129, which leave east to PR-11. D-031 names this, and the count stays 26
  because V-401 cleared. It belongs to the open lifting gate (design#1 b), not a new finding.
- **Weights (design#4, closed by design-r2).** Recalculated E-501 independently: 15.5 MPa,
  D 1.8 m, allowable about 138 MPa, so t = P D / (2 S E - 1.2 P) is about 0.11-0.12 m. The shell
  is pi x 1.8 x 0.12 x 12 x 7850 = about 64 t, times the script's 2.5, plus heads, which gives
  167.5 t. This is consistent. The lifting study now has load moments up to about 4,400 t·m (R-102
  at 14 m, E-502/E-504 at 22-29 m). A pad and a 40 m reach do not prove such lifts, because no
  crane is chosen. The gate's crane-chart condition stays with design#1 (b).
- **Zones (layout#4).** Hydrogen zone 2 is now at the loop flanges and valves, at X-601, and
  ammonia at V-701/E-701 (drawn as flammable gas, conservative). The rebuild reports no zone
  finding. The basis "about 63 mol% H2" was spot-checked against the stream table: S42, the
  converter feed, has H2 0.635 at 15,400 kPa(a). Confirmed. Still missing: the H-101 fuel and
  tail-gas trains. D-031 says they have "no placed item to be a source", but the zones schema
  (`cheme schema zones`, ZoneSource.item) takes a placed tag, a nozzle `TAG/N` or a **line
  number**. FG-1002 (fuel NG, DN 80) and FG-1010 (tail gas, DN 125) to H-101 are in the line list
  and routed. So the stated reason does not hold, and layout#4 stays open for this remainder.
- **Take-off (layout#3).** D-031 now says what the take-off leaves out: classes and insulation,
  the CW headers, the furnace ducts, small bore, supports and field-run vents and drains. It says
  an estimate must carry these as allowances. That answers half of the action. pipe-specs.yaml and
  the line-list classes and insulation are deferred to S7. It stays open, medium.
- **New pins.** PC-1048 and PC-1084 were pinned by hand to clear the A-800 lines and repeat the
  layout#8 pattern (finding layout-r2#1).
- **Sources spot-checked.**
  1. Converter feed H2 fraction in the Z-HAZ-LOOP basis: S42 has 0.635. Confirmed (above).
  2. Latent heat of water at 30 °C (CW price premise, D-031): steam tables give about 2,430
     kJ/kg. Confirmed, as in design-r2.
  No other external data was added in the fix round.
- **No third-party content** in the changed files. Routes, plans and tables are generated by
  cheme from the project's own YAML.

the remaining items need a user or orchestrator decision: accept lanes with the lifting plan's
reason, move the estimate, or move the maturity requirement. Merge pending: not merged, no
