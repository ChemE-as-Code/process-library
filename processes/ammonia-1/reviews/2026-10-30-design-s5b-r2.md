## The S5b items, checked

1. **maturity-balanced, still failing.** Finding s5b#1 is closed the way the round-1 record
   allowed. H2_COMB and NH3_COMB are back to `assumed` with the inference written out. D-034
   names the exact remaining requirement: a published source for fuel H2 and NH3 burnout in an
   excess-air fired heater. `sourced.boundary` fails for the reason round 1 accepted as factual:
   S1, S12/S61 and S26/S70 are results of this design. It needs the owner's feed specification
   or an adopted reference-plant basis. Both are owner decisions, so they are listed under the
   plan's stop rules. No provenance was relabelled to pass.
2. **lifting-study, 11 -> 8 lanes, by design.** E-301, E-303 and V-701 were cleared by a
   further pad and by re-pinning AL-1129 and DW-1073 (layout-s5b#1). The 8 left are blocked by
   the item's own piping or the radiant box (H-101), or by adjacent piping (E-104, E-402,
   E-502). These are erection-sequence cases. They are proposed to the owner in
   `build/lifting-acceptance-proposal.md`, and none is in an accepted list. Still no crane
   chart (finding r2#2).
3. **Class 4 estimate, not reached; class 5 declared.** The CEPCI is sourced and correct (see
   below). The equipment estimate exists but prices only 14 of 52 items, at purchased cost
   only. D-035 states why: no exchanger areas, no materials, K-101, K-501, C-301 and C-302
   outside the correlations' ranges, and H-101 not simulated. These are honest and stated.
   Calling the result class 5 is correct. The bulk estimate is still absent because rates.yaml
   is the example, and D-035's reason for that is too broad (finding r2#1). The TEA is
   unchanged, still on the scaled IEAGHG CAPEX of D-023. That is consistent, since the
   bottom-up estimate does not cover the plant.
4. **Other findings.** s5b#3 and s5b#4 are closed. s5b#2 has a partial answer: the script maps
   Q8/Q10/Q13/Q23 one to one, so the jump came from DWSIM's exchanger duties. It stays open
   until the EBTF case is rerun. s5b#5 is not done: the PR body on GitHub is still the S5
   round-0 text.

### Spot-checks of cited values

- `estimate/cost-basis.yaml:13-16`: CEPCI annual 2023 = 797.9, Chemical Engineering,
  Sept. 2024, p. 45. I downloaded the cited PDF and extracted its text. The "Annual Index"
  list reads 2022 = 816.0 and 2023 = 797.9. **Confirmed.**
- `estimate/equipment-cost.csv` V-101: I recomputed the Turton vertical-vessel correlation by
  hand (K1 3.4974, K2 0.4485, K3 0.1074) at 107.6 m3. It gives log10 Cp0 = 4.852, so
  Cp0 = 71.1 k USD (2001), which matches the 71,128 in the file. The escalation ratio
  797.9/397 = 2.0098 also matches. **Confirmed.**
- R-501 draft datasheet: the 294 t/h converter feed is plausible for 1500 t/d of NH3 (62.5 t/h)
  at a per-pass conversion of roughly 20 %. Its sources trace to the simulated stream S42.
