## Engineering checks

- **Rack fill and topology.** PR-1 to the east battery limit is reached from A-500/A-700 only
  through PR-6 (x = 164) or PR-10 (x = 135): there is no connector from PR-7 to PR-1 on the east
  side (layout#2).
- **Weights.** The screening method (thin wall, envelope densities) is stated and all values are
  ASSUMED. Spot recalculation of E-501 (converter feed/effluent exchanger, D 1.8 m, about 15 MPa
  in service) at its real pressure gives a shell of about 0.12 m and roughly 140 t with the
  script's own factor 2.5, against the 13.0 t listed (taken at the 1000 kPa(a) default). This is
  2026-10-29-design#4, carried below; it also means the lifting study's crane loads for the loop
  and shift exchangers are low. R-501 at 658 t and C-301 at 514 t are plausible for this
  capacity.
- **Zones.** Radii come from the package table and are marked screening/ASSUMED. Only the
  compressor seals and three natural-gas flanges are sources (layout#4).
- **MTO.** 10,875 m of pipe, 1,225 elbows, 539 t of steel. All pipe is `bare` with no class
  (layout#3). The DN 1400 CW headers and the furnace ducts are not in it.
- **Lifting.** 13 pads with a 40 m reach and 12 laydowns. E-401 has no pad within reach
  (layout/lifting-study.md:32, `no crane pad`). DESIGN.md:828 says "every heavy item has a pad and
  a laydown" (layout#1).
- **Sources spot-checked** (the S5 work adds no external data, so two TEA values that the enabled
  gate rests on were checked):
  1. NH3 price, USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026, Nitrogen: 2025 estimate 450 USD per short
     ton f.o.b. Gulf Coast. **Confirmed** (pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-nitrogen.pdf).
     450 x 1.10231 = 496.0 USD/t; at 1.13 USD/EUR this gives 439 EUR/t, as stated.
  2. Electricity, Eurostat nrg_pc_205 NL band IG, 2025-S1 0.1062 and 2025-S2 0.0951 EUR/kWh.
     **Not confirmed**: band IG was not in the search results. A Statista summary of the same
     table gives 0.2046 EUR/kWh for band IC (500-2,000 MWh) in 2025-S1. A lower IG price is
     consistent with that. The arithmetic mean, 100.65 EUR/MWh, is correct.
- **No third-party content** was found copied into the changed files. The routes, figures and
  renders are generated by cheme.
