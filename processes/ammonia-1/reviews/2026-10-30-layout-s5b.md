## Engineering checks

- **Pads and laydowns in plan.** I wrote a scratch check (`build/review_pads.py`, run with
  `cheme run`). It overlays all 27 pads and 14 laydowns on equipment bounding boxes, structure
  footprints, racks and roads (centreline ± half width), hazardous-zone volumes, pipes below
  4 m, and each other. No pad overlaps any of them, and no pad lies in a hazardous zone.
  D-033's "open ground" claim holds for the pads. One laydown does not hold it: **LD-204**
  (x 156-164) reaches 2 m into access road R-5 (x 152-158). This is finding 2.
- **PG-1120 via.** The route is 250.5 -> 246 at 9.6 m, then north to the header at y = 77.2
  (plant-model.yaml:24379). It no longer passes over V-501, the V-501 lane is clear, and the
  build reports no clash. **Closed by design.** The relief line still rises 6.6 m from the PSV
  outlet to 9.6 m. That is the D-031 arrangement already reviewed, and its self-draining is a
  detail-stage question (relief, S8).
- **The 11 remaining lanes: is the erection sequence really the only answer?** I re-ran D-033's
  own scratch tool (`cheme run build/tools/pad_search.py why <tag>`) for E-301, E-303, E-402,
  E-104 and V-701, and read the blocking routes in plant-model.yaml:
  - **V-701 (6.6 t).** A pad 1 m south of the vessel, at [316, 59.5, 324, 67.5], lies on open
    ground. Its lane is blocked only by **AL-1129**. AL-1129 is a DN 25 line from X-601
    (line-list.yaml:247), not V-701's own outlet. The router runs it north at x = 321 m,
    8.85 m up, along the crown of V-701 (shell x 318.5-321.5, top 8 m), before it drops to
    T1129 at grade (plant-model.yaml:24650). A via a few metres west or east of the vessel
    would clear the lane. The proposal calls AL-1129 "its own outlet piping", which is not
    correct.
  - **E-303 (7.2 t) and E-301 (7.9 t).** **DW-1073** (DN 25 demineralised water make-up to
    V-302) leaves PR-4 at x = 208.7 m and runs north at 8.85 m from y = 164 to 192
    (plant-model.yaml:23314). That run passes directly over E-301, E-302 and E-303. DW-1073 is
    in every lane the tool tries for E-303 (eight pad positions) and in all but one for E-301.
    It is a router choice for a small utility line, and the plan names re-pinning lane blockers
    as the first resort. E-303 also has PC-1071/PC-1072 in its CP-202 lane, so a re-pin alone
    may not clear it, but nobody has tried.
  - **E-402.** PG-1056 is in every lane. It is a process line between C-301 and E-401, so
    re-routing it is a larger change. Proposing the erection sequence here is reasonable.
  - **E-104, H-101, K-501, E-501, E-502, E-505, E-504.** The blockers are the item's own piping,
    its radiant tubes, or the adjacent exchanger's piping in a dense nest. Proposing the
    erection sequence for these is reasonable.

  So D-033 says "no pad position clears them; only the erection sequence does", but for at least
  V-701, and probably E-301/E-303, the blocker is a small routed line that a via would move.
  That is finding 1. It does not change the verdict on its own. The gate would still fail on
  the other lanes, which need the owner.
- **Crane capacity.** There is no crane chart, so no lift is checked against a capacity. R-501
  (658 t, 1,013 t at 21.9 m) and C-301 (514 t, 792 t at 25.1 m) are named as 1,000 t class
  crawler lifts. That is plausible for these weights and radii, but it is not demonstrated.
  This is part of design#1 (b).
- **Weights.** I compared the lifting table with the equipment list for R-501 (658.1 t),
  C-301 (514.3 t) and K-501 (235.2 t). The values match. Their basis (screening weights at
  flowsheet pressures, design#5) was closed in design-r2.
- **Spot-check of cited values.** S5b cites no published value in the layout files: the pads,
  laydowns and lifting criteria are all marked ASSUMED, which is honest. I tried two of the new
  design citations, AP-42 §1.4 Table 1.4-2 footnote b (EPA server: HTTP 403) and Lin et al.
  2024, doi:10.1016/j.eng.2024.06.003 (publisher redirect, no text). Neither could be opened,
  so I did not verify either. design-s5b confirmed EP 1586535 A2 and BREF LVIC-AAF §2.2.3.6.
- **Third-party content.** The proposal and D-033 are in our own words. Nothing is copied.

## Verdict

**fix.** S5b's layout work is real and clean. 13 pads and 2 laydowns lie on open ground and
outside the hazardous zones. The PG-1120 via clears V-501, the model is current and clash-free,
and nothing was accepted. But the section's gates do not pass. lifting-study fails with 11
lanes, maturity-balanced fails on `sourced.boundary`, and there is no class 4 estimate, so the
high finding 2026-10-29-design#1 stays open. At least one of the 11 lanes (V-701), and probably
E-301/E-303, can still be cleared by re-routing a DN 25 line, which is agent work (s5b#1). The
rest need the owner's decision on the erection sequence and the feed basis (runs/plan.md stop
rules). Merge pending: not merged, no sign-off.
