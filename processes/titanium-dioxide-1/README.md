A plant design kept as plain files in git: DEXPI 2.0.1 P&IDs in `pid/`, engineering lists as
YAML, decisions and progress as Markdown. Edited by people and by coding agents (Claude Code),
checked by [`cheme-as-code`](https://github.com/ChemE-as-Code/core).

Titanium dioxide by the chloride process, as the EU LVIC-S BREF (2007) describes it: a blend of
titanium slag and synthetic rutile (88 wt% TiO2) and calcined petroleum coke are chlorinated in a
fluidised bed at 1273 K, TiO2 + 2 Cl2 + 3/2 C -> TiCl4 + CO + 1/2 CO2, the impurities to metal
chlorides. The gas is quenched with cold TiCl4, the blow-over and metal chlorides leave by a
cyclone, and the TiCl4 is condensed (cooling water, then brine), distilled, vaporised, preheated
and oxidised with 15 % excess oxygen: TiCl4 + O2 -> TiO2 + 2 Cl2. The pigment is cooled and
collected in a bag filter, and the chlorine with the oxygen excess goes straight back to the
chlorinator (the direct recycle). The CO/CO2 off-gas is scrubbed with water and caustic and its CO
burnt in a thermal oxidiser; the blow-over, the still sludge and the scrubber liquor are
neutralised with lime and filtered. 100 kt/a of raw TiO2 at 8000 h; the finishing plant is
outside the boundary.

Core solves the whole mass balance: the chlorine loop is torn at the recycle gas S4 and
converged together with six design specifications (the feedstock for the product rate; the coke,
make-up chlorine, oxygen, caustic and lime for their ratios), with component separators by
specified recovery and conversion reactors with solid components. The energy balance with the
solids and liquid TiCl4 comes from `scripts/energy_balance.py` on the `chemicals`/`thermo` data,
because core has no condensed-phase enthalpy in a flowsheet step (DESIGN.md L-1). Needs
cheme-as-code 1.12.

Headline numbers and their caveats:
- Per t of raw TiO2: 1.214 t feedstock (1.069 t TiO2 in it), 316 kg coke, 128 kg make-up
  chlorine, 462 kg oxygen, 102 kg quicklime, 31 kg of 50 wt% caustic; 982 kg fossil CO2 to air,
  396 kg filter cake to landfill. Each lies inside the range the EU chloride sites reported in
  the BREF (DESIGN.md D-012), which the assumptions aimed at: plausibility, not prediction.
- The oxidiser's adiabatic outlet is 1997 K with TiCl4 preheated to 723 K and oxygen to 1273 K
  (the BREF gives 900-2000 C); the chlorinator is autothermal with a 1.0 MW surplus lost through
  its shell.
- Steam 1.72 GJ/t and fired heat 0.77 GJ/t are the thermal minimum of this flowsheet, below the
  BREF's 1999 averages (2.4 and 3.2 GJ/t), which include drying, an abandoned chlorine recovery
  and furnace losses. Electricity is the BREF's 1999 site average (1.12 kWh/kg with the air fan).
- The LCI is preliminary: the titanium feedstock (slag or synthetic rutile, the main upstream
  burden) and the coke have no library producer and stay gaps.

## How this repository works

- **This repository holds your plant**: drawings, lists, decisions. It does not hold the tools.
- **The tools come from [`cheme-as-code`](https://github.com/ChemE-as-Code/core)**, installed once on
  your computer (`uv tool install git+https://github.com/ChemE-as-Code/core.git`). Every check,
  drawing and generated file is made by the `cheme` command, so you need it to work here.
- **Licences.** This folder is part of the ChemE-as-Code process library. Its data and designs are
  under CC BY 4.0 (`LICENSE-DATA`, unless `process.yaml` states otherwise); its skills and scripts
  are under AGPL-3.0-or-later (`LICENSE`), like the ChemE-as-Code tools. It is public
  (`publication` in `process.yaml`: public, CC-BY-4.0).
- **The skills in `.claude/skills/` are not in git.** They come with cheme-as-code: run
  `cheme skills update` once in this folder after cloning, so that Claude Code finds them here.

## Working here

```sh
cheme validate                       # after every change
cheme check                          # engineering-step gates (--write only at sign-off)
cheme process propagate --fill       # flows, compositions, the chlorine recycle and the design specifications
cheme run scripts/energy_balance.py  # temperatures, air and duties (update the flowsheet's values it cites)
cheme process build                  # BFD and PFD from process/*.yaml
cheme process streams                # stream table with provenance, block balances
cheme assess lca --write             # the preliminary LCI
```
