- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:oxidiser-nox (NOx of the thermal oxidiser and of the fired preheaters not estimated (BREF LVIC-S Table 3.19: 0.10 kg NOx per t from chlorination, 1999 average); the fired heaters' combustion emissions are in the background of the fired-heat entry.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:dust (Dust from the raw material import and preparation (ore and coke handling, closed systems with cyclones and filters; BREF LVIC-S 3.2.2.1 and Table 3.18: 0.002 kg per t, 1999 average) not in the balance: the feedstock handling is not drawn and has no P&ID or abatement design yet. The in-boundary filters do not vent: the pigment bag filter F-201 returns its gas to the chlorinator (S4) and the cyclone S-101 passes its gas to the condensers. The BREF's oxidation-stage table (3.22) reports no dust; its finishing-section dust (Table 3.23) is outside the boundary (D-002).)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:waste-tanks (The lime slaker (about 366 K, adiabatic) and the neutralisation tank (323 K, cooled) are aqueous tanks holding no volatile component in the balance (the TiCl4 and HCl are neutralised); their vapour is water (D-010). The slaker's water vapour is not in the balance: open for basic engineering.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:fugitive-chlorine (No P&ID yet: fugitive chlorine, TiCl4 and CO from flanges, valves and seals cannot be counted; the black end is totally enclosed (BREF LVIC-S 3.2.2.1). Open for basic engineering.)
