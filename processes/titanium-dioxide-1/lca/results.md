## Limitations

- construction (infrastructure, equipment) not included
- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:cos-so2 (The coke's sulfur (0.8-1.8 wt%, BREF LVIC-S 3.2.3.4.2) leaves as COS, burnt to SO2 in the thermal oxidiser: not modelled (the coke is carried as carbon only, L-3). BREF LVIC-S Table 3.19 reports 2.39 kg COS and 1.14 kg SO2 per t from chlorination (1999 averages): open for the next stage.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:oxidiser-nox (NOx of the thermal oxidiser and of the fired preheaters not estimated (BREF LVIC-S Table 3.19: 0.10 kg NOx per t from chlorination, 1999 average); the fired heaters' combustion emissions are in the background of the fired-heat entry.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:dust (Dust from raw material handling and from the pigment filters (BREF LVIC-S Tables 3.18 and 3.23: 0.002 and 0.158 kg per t, 1999 averages) not in the balance: no P&ID or abatement design yet.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:waste-tanks (The lime slaker and the neutralisation tank are open aqueous tanks at 313-323 K holding no volatile component in the balance (the TiCl4 and HCl are neutralised); their vapour is water: negligible.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:fugitive-chlorine (No P&ID yet: fugitive chlorine, TiCl4 and CO from flanges, valves and seals cannot be counted; the black end is totally enclosed (BREF LVIC-S 3.2.2.1). Open for basic engineering.)
