## Limitations

- construction (infrastructure, equipment) not included
- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:fugitive-nh3 (Not estimated at concept stage: no P&ID take-off for a leak count. BREF LVIC-AAF Table 8.11 reports diffuse NH3 of about 10-12 t/a for Dutch urea plants, of the order of 0.02 g/kg urea at this capacity: small next to the 0.77 g/kg of the vent and exhaust, to be estimated at basic engineering)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:storage (No tanks inside the boundary: ammonia storage and urea storage/handling are outside it (D-001))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:wastewater (No untreated wastewater leaves: the process condensate is desorbed and hydrolysed to 1 ppm(wt) NH3 and urea (BAT for new plants, BREF Table 8.20) and re-used as boiler feed water; rinsing and cleaning water is not in the concept balance)
