## Process and balance results
- **Route:** one adiabatic Pd/alumina fixed bed with a large hydrogen recycle (60 mol H₂ per mol nitrobenzene at the inlet, 513 → 729 K, 5 bar). The effluent raises steam, is condensed and separated into gas, wet crude aniline and process water; a dehydration column, a vacuum aniline column and a water stripper do the work-up.
- **Mass:** every block and step balance closes in cheme. Plant in 34,908.5 kg/h, out 34,908.1 kg/h. Carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen and oxygen close to 1e-5 kmol/h.
- **Reactor energy:** cheme's adiabatic reactor duty comes out at −0.8 kW, which confirms the script's 729 K outlet. The equilibrium constant is far too large for equilibrium to limit conversion.
- **Plant energy:** closes to 99.7 % in the script. The 152 kW left over is exactly the pump work, which the script does not turn into heat. cheme itself cannot evaluate energy closure for these streams (no property package).
- **Column steam:** 0.41 kWh/kg, inside the range of the open Covestro patent's examples scaled to this size.
