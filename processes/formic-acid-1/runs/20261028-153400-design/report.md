## Route and key numbers
- **Route:** CO and methanol react over sodium methoxide (353 K, 45 bar) to methyl formate. The ester is hydrolysed with water at 393 K, with recycled formic acid as catalyst. A 203 kPa column and a 12 kPa column then concentrate the acid to 85 wt% (pressure-swing distillation across the azeotrope). I chose this over extraction so the route doesn't need the extraction model, which core qualifies only for another solvent system. The CO-rich vent is burnt.
- **Capacity:** 6.25 t/h of 85 wt% acid (50 kt/a), assumed.
- **Hydrolysis:** 28.9 % per pass. This comes from the patents' equilibrium constant (0.20) at 95 % approach, and sits inside the 28–32 % the patents report. Core's own equilibrium check confirms the reactor outlet is just short of equilibrium.

## Sources used
- **UCB patent US4216339A:** carbonylation conditions, 30 % conversion, 92 % CO yield, water limits.
- **Taminco patent EP2747883A1:** hydrolysis conditions, equilibrium constant 0.18–0.24, vacuum column at 10–13 kPa with 63–66 % bottoms, and a prior-art figure of 2400 kWh/t used only as a cross-check.
- **Eastman patent EP3661904B1:** azeotrope composition versus pressure, the pressure-swing scheme, conversions of 25–32 %.
- **Lighter use:** US4299981 (only the claims shown in search results) and MDPI Catalysts 11 (2021) 869 (corroboration only, not read in full).
- **Component data:** cheme's thermo and props packages.
- **Not used:** Ullmann (not read; licensed). No values from licensed databases.

## Key assumed values
CO purity 99 mol%, water/ester ratio 1.5, column splits and compositions (96 / 98 wt% ester, 77 / 85 / 66 wt% acid), reflux ratios 1.0–1.5, 1 % catalyst purge, heat of hydrolysis +8 kJ/mol, 99.9 % oxidiser destruction, and the pressure profile.

The shortcut steam duty comes out about 1.5 times the patent's prior-art figure. That is plausible with no heat integration, and I kept it as a conservative value rather than tuning it down.
