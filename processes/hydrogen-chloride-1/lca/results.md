## Boundary check

No warning from the boundary check, whose warnings are that:

- steps of the unit kinds compressor, pump, heater, cooler, shortcut_column, conversion_reactor, equilibrium_reactor, gibbs_reactor have an energy stream at a port (or are declared adiabatic) (warning when not)
- every product terminal has a category (warning when not)

It notes (info, not a gap):

- every feed terminal has a category
- energy terminals are classified utility; heat utilities declare a kind and supply temperature

It does not check:

- steps of other unit kinds (component separators, flash drums, mixers, splitters, unknown classes)
- whether a step's energy stream reaches a utility terminal (a step-to-step stream counts)
- whether duties are given or balance (cheme process energy)

## Limitations

- gate-to-gate: no upstream supply chain
- construction (infrastructure, equipment) not included
- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:furnace-combustion (R101 burns hydrogen in chlorine: no air and no carbon enter the flame (chlor-alkali-1's chlorine carries O2 only), so no NOx, CO, CO2, CH4, N2O or particulates form; its products are all in the balance (S3). Real cell chlorine carries traces of N2 and CO2, not modelled (D-003))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:non-routine (start-up (purging, ignition with excess hydrogen), shutdown and burner trips send hydrogen and HCl/Cl2-bearing gas to the scrubber and vent; not estimated at the concept stage (D-013))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:tanks (acid storage is outside the boundary (D-001); its breathing is normally scrubbed (ASSUMED))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:leaks (fugitive Cl2, H2 and HCl from flanges and seals are not estimated at the concept stage; to be added with the P&IDs)
