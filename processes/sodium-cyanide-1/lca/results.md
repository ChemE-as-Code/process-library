Recorded as an open gap for basic engineering, not as negligible (HCN is acutely toxic).)

Open gap for the next stage, not negligible.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:vents-and-dust (The crystalliser vacuum vent (NH3 and HCN from NaCN hydrolysis, which forms the formate of real products) and the dryer/briquetting dust filter (NaCN particulate) emit traces not in the flowsheet; amounts not known at concept stage. Open gap.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:start-up-and-flaring (Converter light-off, gauze changes, trips and relief send HCN-bearing gas to a flare; not part of the steady design case and no flare is designed at concept stage. Open gap.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:storage (Ammonia, caustic and intermediate HCN/NaCN solution tanks (breathing to a scrubber) are not designed at concept stage; liquid HCN is not stored (fed directly to R-401). Open gap.)
