- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:so2-dosing (AP-42 6.5 reports small SO2 additions to the reactor feed to keep the catalyst active and 4.7 kg SO2 per Mg of PA in the main vent; the promoted catalysts of US 8 859 459 B2 and US 7 592 293 B2 are run without it in their examples, so no SO2 is dosed in this design (D-005); a plant that doses SO2 adds that emission.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:oxidiser-nox-startup (Thermal NOx of the regenerative oxidiser and its start-up and support fuel are not estimated: no sourced factor read (D-009).)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:purification-vents (The pre-treatment vapours, the columns' vacuum-pump discharges and the tank breathing go to the oxidiser in the plant (AP-42 6.5: pre-treatment and distillation emissions are routed to the main control device); their flows are not in the balance (no P&ID yet, D-009). AP-42 6.5's uncontrolled factors, 6.4 and 45 kg/Mg, would add about 2.6 kg/Mg after 95 % destruction.)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:catalyst-change (Catalyst replacement (V2O5/TiO2 rings every few years) and its disposal or vanadium recovery are not counted: no open catalyst life or mass read (D-012).)
