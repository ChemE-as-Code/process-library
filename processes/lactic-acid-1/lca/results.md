- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:ethanol-vents (No P&ID yet: the vents of the ethanol-bearing columns, reactors and drums (and the vacuum pumps of C-401 and E-404) carry ethanol vapour; to a vent condenser, not estimated at the concept stage (D-012).)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:lime-and-acid-vents (The lime slaker gives off steam and lime dust and the acidulation tank holds a hot slurry with traces of acetic acid; vented to a scrubber or dust filter, not estimated at the concept stage (D-012).)
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:fermenter-vents (The fermenters breathe sterile air at filling and harvest; homofermentative lactic acid bacteria give off little gas and the model carries none (D-005, limit L-3); not estimated.)
