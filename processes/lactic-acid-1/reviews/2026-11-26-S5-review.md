## Verdict

The S4b findings are closed. The fermenter typicals read as an industrial typical overview:
16 fermenters on common headers, harvest to an agitated, warm hold tank, and variable-speed
feed to the centrifuges. The arithmetic of T-201 and the harvest holds.

The weak points:
- The typicals exist only on the sheets. Their instruments, relief devices and trips are in
  no list (#1).
- T-201 is drawn without the protection any atmospheric tank needs (#2).
- Of the stage task, P-205 with its S3#1 to #5 precondition and P-204 are not drawn.

No critical or high finding is open. **Merge pending**: a human merges after reading this
record. Nothing was signed off.
