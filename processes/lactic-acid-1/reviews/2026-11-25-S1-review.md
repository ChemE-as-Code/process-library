  - The agitator: 0.0985 kW/m3 x 1291 m3 = 127 kW.
  - The coil duty and area: 447 and 670 kW, an LMTD of 11.1 K, 152 m2.

  - Every new number is either sourced or marked ASSUMED.
- D-027 is confirmed by this review. Inventing a NUT formula would make an unsourced nitrogen
  balance look sourced; the mass balances close.

The decisions read like an industrial lactic-acid plant on lime. These choices are sound and
well argued:
- the schedule-driven number of seed trains;
- campaign sterilisation with the reason the sterile buffer was rejected;
- the tempered-water loop against cold-wall crystallisation;
- no aeration, from SRC-1;
- an N2 overlay;
- steam blocks marking the sterile boundary.

The findings below are gaps in the physics and hydraulics that P-101, P-201 and P-202 would
otherwise draw in.
