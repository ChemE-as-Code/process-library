## What was checked

- `cheme validate`: 0 errors and 11 warnings. Five are drawing warnings on P-103: 2
  line-crossing, 1 line-through-label and 2 offpage-not-on-edge. Four are
  instrument-no-interlock warnings for P-202 trip switches (TSHH-2012A, FSLL-2014A,
  LSHH-2032A, PALL-2041A); P-202 is a later sheet, so these are expected until its C&E
  entries exist. The last two are review-finding-id/status warnings on the S1 record's
  placeholder row "-" (cosmetic). P-101 and P-102 have zero drawing findings.
- `cheme fmt --check`: clean.
- `cheme check --stage basic`: the following gates fail:
  - drawing-clean (the 5 P-103 warnings, not accepted);
  - design-review (S1 record stale; this record replaces it);
  - layout-plot-plan, which passed at S1 and now fails because P-108A/B are not placed;
  - layout-3d, lifting-study, mto and layout-review, which belong to the later layout stage.
- Renders: P-101, P-102 and P-103 as full PNGs, plus crops of P-103 (sterile loop with
  X-101, P-108A/B and the divert; E-101/E-103/P-104; E-102/X-101) and P-102 (R-101, T-105,
  P-106/107). What I say about the drawings comes from these images. I looked at P-101 only
  at full-sheet resolution: it is readable, with storage, day tanks, blend and V-101 in two
  rows.
- D-015 arithmetic, all consistent:
  - 200 t/h x 4.18 kJ/(kg K) = 232 kW/K. E-101 duty is 232 x 90 K = about 21 MW. E-102
    duty is 232 x 16.4 K = 3.8 MW (1.47 MW at the 77 t/h year average, Q17).
  - Approach is 16.4 K at both ends.
  - X-101 holds 6.3 m3 (120 s at 189 m3/h). DN 250 gives 1.07 m/s and about 130 m of tube.
  - Holding pressure 3.5 barg against saturation at 2.6 barg (413 K).
  - Pressure profile: sterile side 6.4 barg against raw side 5.4 barg. The booster
    develops 3.6 bar.
  - Re-sterilisation steam: 2.5 to 3 MWh per 9.88 h campaign, about 0.3 MW average.
  - Campaign rate: 761 t in 4 h = 190 t/h, against 77 t/h year average.
  - All conditions are marked ASSUMED, and the extra steam is in Open inputs.
