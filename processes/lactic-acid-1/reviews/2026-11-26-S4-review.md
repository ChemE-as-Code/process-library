## What the sheet shows (as seen in the renders)

One large vessel in the middle. The inlets come in from the left: medium, inoculum, tempered-water loop with P-201A. The
vent, relief, N2/vacuum break, sampling and lime enter from the right. CIP supply and SIP steam come from the top; the bottom
cluster takes harvest, CIP return and bottom SIP steam. Each sterile entry has a visible steam
block: two valves, steam in, and a trap to drain. Five notes give the typical, NOT AERATED, the
STERILE BOUNDARY, the split range with the trips, and the filter bypass. Line labels are
legible at full-sheet size. The sterile boundary design is sound and reads like an industrial
fermenter: medium, inoculum, lime, CIP supply/return and harvest all have steam blocks, and
there is a steamed sampling valve, filtered N2 and a vent with a back-pressure valve. The
weaknesses are in the drawing: valve tags, the agitator and the coils.

## Verdict

The engineering of R-201A is at basic-engineering level and sound for an industrial anaerobic
fermenter:
- the sterile boundary has steam blocks on every entry, including CIP supply and return;
- an independent high-high level trip;
- split-range heating and cooling;
- an N2 overlay with a self-acting make-up;
- a named, prevented steam-collapse vacuum case with an honest HAZOP hold;
- a SIP-inclusive turnaround.

The numbers check, and assumptions are marked.

The sheet is not yet ready to show: valve tags are missing (S4#1, high), and the agitator and
coils are not drawn as such (S4#2, S4#3). S4#1 to S4#3 are drawing fixes of a few hours, with
`cheme set`, `fragment label` and `move`. S4#4 and S4#5 are short additions to D-031 and the
C&E. **Merge pending**: not before S4#1 is closed.
