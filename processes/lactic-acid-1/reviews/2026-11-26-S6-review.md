## What reads well

- P-301 shows the three centrifuges, each with its own feed FIC. The cake goes to hopper
  V-301 and the PC pumps to AD, and the clarified broth to R-301 through FT-3020.
- Acid control is ratio FFIC-3021 on FT-3020, trimmed by AIC-3024.
- R-301 is cooled by a pump-around through the spiral E-301 with TIC-3023 on the CW. The
  slurry goes on a VFD with no control valve.
- The belt filter has its vacuum receiver, liquid-ring pumps and filtrate pumps.
- The notes on the sheet state the basis and the ASSUMED values.
- P-401 shows a recognisable four-effect forward-feed falling-film train: circulation pumps,
  vapour cascade, surface condenser, condensate receiver under the vacuum pumps, and
  concentrate drawn on density.
- D-038 records the change of acid injection point against D-025 with its reason.
