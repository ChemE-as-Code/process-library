    about 3.5 barg.
  - Line hold-up: 250 m x 7.9 L/m is about 2 m3.

## Verdict

P-201 is a credible basic-engineering seed train. Each vessel is agitated and jacketed, with
temperature, pH, level and pressure. It has sterile inlet and vent filters, steam blocks at the
sterile entries, CIP/SIP clusters and a sterile transfer chain to the inoculum header. The D-030
numbers check. Nothing is critical or high. Close the four medium findings before P-205 copies
train A: #1 relief case, #2 sampling, #3 R-213B/C design pressure, and #5 together with S2#1 on
the CIP and sterile-header single seats. Otherwise each fix has to be made three times. The
untracked `pid/P-201.refused.xml` is a stray scratch file to delete; it is not part of this
review.
