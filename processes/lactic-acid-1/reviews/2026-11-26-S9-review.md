## Fermentation area

S9 changed no fermentation sheet. As an industrial bioprocess engineer would read it, the
fermentation area is unchanged from the S5 to S8 reviews:

- The concept is sound: a five-stage seed train in three trains, a continuous steriliser with
  a holding tube and a divert, R-201A drawn in full as the typical with pH, temperature,
  level, pressure, the sterile vent filter, vacuum protection, SIP and CIP, and an anaerobic
  statement.
- These are still open:
  - the instruments and trips of the typicals R-201B to P in the lists (S5#1);
  - the T-201 vent, overflow and LAHH (S5#2);
  - the CIP return divert and permissives (S5b#2, #3);
  - the single seat at the sterile boundary at XV-1025A (S2#1);
  - the 3D fermenter deck (S8#1 to #4).

The "Basic-engineering status" section of DESIGN.md is a fair summary of all of this, and its
intended fixes for S8#1 to #3 match what the S8 review asked for. Every new number in D-044 is
either from the stream table or marked ASSUMED.

## Verdict

S9 did what its time limit allowed, and reported it honestly:

- XV-3013 is confirmed on P-301, so S6#1 is closed.
- P-404 is drawn, readable and in step with P-403's connectors and the lists.
- The overfill trips of the product and ethanol tanks are in the C&E.

P-404 is not at the level of the other downstream sheets:

- the trip and control valves are untagged, and the acceptance of the trip valves rests on a
  label that is not drawn (#1);
- there is no pump isolation (#4);
- the ethanol blanketing is not drawn (#3).

The layout model is stale at HEAD (#2). S8#1 (high) remains open, so the branch cannot be
signed off or merged. Merge pending.
