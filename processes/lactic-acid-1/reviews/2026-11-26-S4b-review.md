at c49d86cd) fixes P-202 against S4#1 to S4#8, records D-033, extends I-204, rewrites
`process/recipe.yaml` to 12 h and replaces the P-201 wildcard acceptances (S3#6).

## What was checked

- `cheme validate`: 0 errors, 67 warnings. The P-202 drawing warnings are 2 `line-crossing`
  (the TIC-2011A outputs over 150-TW-2023A), 2 `label-far-from-owner` (100-CS-2016A) and
  8 `offpage-not-on-edge` (steam and flush stubs). All 12 are accepted one by one in
  `checks/checks.yaml`. Not accepted, and not drawing warnings:
  - 8 `instrument-not-on-pid` (TV-2011A/B, AV-2021A, XV-2033A/2034A, PV-2041A, XV-2043A/2044A);
  - `piping-item-not-on-pid` and `label-tag-unknown` for PSE-2045A (see #1).
  Expected warnings:
  - the P-201 and P-103 ones, including P-103's `offpage-mismatch` (S3#9);
  - the S1 record's `-` row;
  - info items for the planned sheets P-203, P-204, P-205 and P-301 to P-403.
- `cheme fmt --check`: clean.
- `cheme check --stage basic`: every gate passes except these:
  - `design-review`: fails until this record is committed;
  - `layout-3d`, `lifting-study`, `mto` and `layout-review`: no plant model and no layout review yet (S8).
- `checks/checks.yaml` has no wildcard left for P-201 (37 explicit ids), so S3#6 is closed.
- Rendered P-202: the full sheet at `--png --scale 1.5`, plus two crops at scale 4:
  - `build/P-202_crop-0-20-250-220.png`: the vessel, the inlets and the tempered-water loop;
  - `build/P-202_crop-180-15-420-230.png`: the vessel, the vent, relief, N2, pH, sampling and CIP return.

  What I saw:
  - Every actuated valve now prints its tag: XV-2033A/2034A, TV-2011A/B, PV-2041A, XV-2043A/2044A, AV-2021A and PSV-2046A.
  - A-201A is a top-entry drive: motor M, gearbox G, a shaft through the top head, seal S and the impeller in the broth.
  - The half-pipe coil outline sits outside the shell, and 150-TW-2022A/2023A end on it.
  - TIC-2011A is clear of the riser. Its two outputs are labelled "TV-2011A: 50-100 % (CW)" and "TV-2011B: 0-50 % (LP steam)".
  - PCV-2042A has an impulse line downstream of its valve and the note "self-acting".
  - PSE-2045A has its XA-2045A burst bubble.
  - PSV-2046A is labelled "vacuum relief -0.2 barg" and admits air through F-202A.
  - The milk-of-lime branch has a steam block, a bleed and a flush with a check valve.
  - The sheet reads as an industrial fermenter. Several notes are very small (#4).
- Arithmetic re-done:
  - D-033 relief load. Steam at about 2.2 kg/m3 with K = 10 and 0.75 bar across gives about 83 m/s. That is 1.5 kg/s in DN 100 plus 0.9 kg/s in DN 80, 2.4 kg/s in total (D-033: 2.5).
  - Napier for saturated steam at 3.75 bar(a) gives about 550 kg/(m2 s), or about 340 with Kd 0.62. Over the DN 150 bore this is about 6.0 to 6.4 kg/s (D-033: 6.7). The margin is more than 2, so DN 150 holds.
  - Recipe: 4.0 + 1.25 + 2.75 + 4.0 = 12.0 h; (146.07 + 12) / 16 = 9.88 h. Both overlaps (pre-rinse during the drain, fill at the end of the cool-down) are usual practice.
- I-204 now stops P-201A and P-221A/B as well as closing TV-2011A and XV-2034A.
