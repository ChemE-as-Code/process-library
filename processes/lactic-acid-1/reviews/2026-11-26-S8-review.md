## Fermentation area

The plot-plan decision (D-026) still holds. It is a sound industrial arrangement: two
outdoor rows of eight at 14 m pitch, the rack between them, crane pads and laydown on the
outer roads, and the reserved area A-250 for more fermenters. The seed trains are now 10 m
apart in a clad building with a 25 t crane for the stage-5 vessels. That is reasonable.

Bioprocess engineers will look at the 3D fermenter rows, and three things there will not
pass:

- The top-deck concept: the drive on independent steel (#2) and the valves under the
  grating (#3).
- The branch-valve access, which generates 216 platforms, and the branch clashes (#1).
- Escape from a 112 m deck at 24.5 m (#4).

Every layout dimension is marked ASSUMED in D-043, structures.yaml, routing.yaml and
zones.yaml. The zones are stated as a screening, not an area classification, which is
correct.
