**Balance and check results**
- **Mass balance:** every block and step closes; the plant takes in and puts out 19,011 kg/h.
- **Yields:** 98.3 mol% of the fresh methanol ends up as methyl chloride. 87.9 % of the chlorine in the fresh HCl reaches the product; 11.7 % leaves in the dilute acid.
- **Reactor:** cheme calculates the outlet from the sourced conversion and selectivity.
  - The main reaction ends at 0.91 of its equilibrium constant, which fits the source's "close to equilibrium" statement.
  - Dimethyl ether (DME) formation ends slightly past its own equilibrium (1.33). I note this in DESIGN.md as within the uncertainty of a single data point.
- **Reactor duty:** cheme's ideal-gas value is −1,510.8 kW against my hand estimate of −1,534 kW. The quench acid strength (20.5 wt% HCl, patent range 20–25 %) and the spent acid strength (80.5 %, patent ≈80 %) come out of the model and agree with the patent. The DME in the crude gas (0.56 mol%, patent range 0.3–2 %) is set by the selectivity value, so it is a check of that number rather than of the separators.
- **Gates:** concept gates are 6 ok; the only failure is the plot plan, and layout is out of scope. `cheme maturity` reaches no level, because the separator splits and the HCl feed spec are assumed and nothing is simulated. `process.yaml` says so.

**Sources used**
- **US 4,794,204** (Hoechst patent on DME removal): crude gas composition, quench acid strength, and drying with 95 % acid that leaves ≈80 % with 5–10 % DME. This is the main primary source.
- **Schmidt et al. 2014** (doi:10.1515/gps-2014-0039): 97.6 % conversion and 98.8 % selectivity at 340 °C. I could only read the abstract.
- **WO 2000/076658:** about equimolar feed, 250–300 °C, 1–10 bar, hot-spot limits.
- **US 3,983,180:** used only to compare against the liquid-phase route.
- **Ullmann's chapter (issue #151 lead):** licensed, not read.

**Key assumed values (marked ASSUMED)**
- **Basis:** 100 kt/a at 8000 h/a.
- **HCl feed:** HCl/methanol ratio 1.10 at the reactor inlet; the HCl contains 0.1 mol% inerts.
- **Reactor:** 523 K in, 613 K out.
- **Separators:** quench gas at 0.5 mol% HCl and water activity 0.7; stripper recovers 99 % of the methanol; spent acid holds 8 wt% DME; HCl column overhead is 70 mol% methyl chloride, with a 10 % purge.
- **Utilities:** all hand estimates. I found no open source of plant utility data for this route, so they are not benchmarked.
