## Boundary check

No warning from the boundary check, whose warnings are that:

- steps of the unit kinds compressor, pump, heater, cooler, shortcut_column, conversion_reactor, equilibrium_reactor, gibbs_reactor have an energy stream at a port (or are declared adiabatic) (warning when not)
- every product terminal has a category (warning when not)

It notes (info, not a gap):

- every feed terminal has a category
- energy terminals are classified utility; heat utilities declare a kind and supply temperature

It does not check:

- steps of other unit kinds (component separators, flash drums, mixers, splitters, unknown classes)
- whether a step's energy stream reaches a utility terminal (a step-to-step stream counts)
- whether duties are given or balance (cheme process energy)

## Limitations

- construction (infrastructure, equipment) not included
- end of life of the plant not included
- transport of supplies not included
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:fugitive-leaks (fugitive methyl chloride, methanol and HCl from flanges, valves, pumps and compressor seals not estimated, not claimed negligible: an estimate needs the P&ID component counts; to become an emission_estimates entry with the P&IDs (D-014))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:storage-tanks (methanol, methyl chloride (pressurised) and acid storage are outside the battery limit (design basis); their breathing losses belong to storage (D-014))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:purge-combustion (the purge is burned in the site thermal oxidiser outside the plant boundary; its combustion emissions belong to the treatment background of product:PURGE (D-014))
- cut off by the study's criteria: emission-excluded:non-routine (catalyst regeneration or decoking off-gas, start-up and relief venting are not in the flowsheet; open item I-05 (D-014))
