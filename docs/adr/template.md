# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as the decision taken

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN (a link to it) | Deprecated, YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who decided (the owner, a maintainer, the coordinator)
- **Scope:** which part of the library it governs (structure, a schema, the LCI pipeline, publication, ...)

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints (licences, Brightway, core's contracts,
the Atlas), and the options that were considered. Write it so that someone who was not there
can follow why a decision was needed.

## Decision

What we decided, stated as rules the library follows ("A process is ...", "The build refuses
..."). Name the files that hold or enforce the rule.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and the limitations and open follow-ups. Include the
negative consequences.

## Evidence

Where the decision can be seen in the repository: commits (short hash), pull requests and
issues (`#NN` for this repository, `core#NN` for ChemE-as-Code/core), and the files and tests
that enforce it.
